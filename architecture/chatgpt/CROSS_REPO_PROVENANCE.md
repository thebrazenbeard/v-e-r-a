# Cross-Repository Provenance

## ON_THEO -> Testament

Testament may consume ON_THEO research only with preserved provenance.

A downstream literary use should retain, where practical:
- ON_THEO repository;
- exact commit SHA;
- artifact path;
- claim/evidence classification;
- uncertainty/dispute note.

Conceptual flow:

`ON_THEO CLAIM -> EVIDENCE CLASS + CONFIDENCE + PROVENANCE -> TESTAMENT LITERARY USE`

Testament may:
- choose among historically possible alternatives;
- invent dialogue;
- create composite characterization;
- compress chronology;
- resolve ambiguity for narrative purposes.

Those choices remain FICTIONAL / LITERARY CONSTRUCTION unless independently
supported upstream.

## Testament -> ON_THEO

The reverse flow is prohibited as evidence.

Testament may trigger:
- a research question;
- a consistency check;
- a request to investigate a literary assumption.

It may not establish:
- historical fact;
- source meaning;
- chronology;
- archaeology;
- theology;
- transmission;
- empirical result.

## Adjacent engineering repos

Outputs from Voss, god-brain, simulations, or other engineering systems must
enter ON_THEO as:
- ANALYSIS RESULT;
- PROJECT INFERENCE;
- SPECULATIVE MODEL;
- EMPIRICAL/SYNTHETIC EXPERIMENT RESULT;

never as primary historical evidence.
