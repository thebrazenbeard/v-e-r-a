## Purpose

The existing evidence ladder already establishes chronology, regional contact, Mesopotamian literary presence at Ugarit, Dumuzi-specific scholastic exposure, and institutional carrier capacity.

Those facts make influence historically possible.

They do not establish that the Baal Cycle borrowed a source-specific Dumuzid death-return sequence.

This pass therefore asks the harder question:

Does the surviving evidence preserve a distinctive, low-base-rate Dumuzid sequence, formula, source marker, or carrier edge inside the Baal Cycle?

## 1. Carrier gate remains partial

PR #73 established a named Ilimilku → Attenu teacher-student network and Ilimilku presence beyond the House of the High Priest.

A newer source-control point strengthens the network without closing the source edge:

RS 94.2406 / KTU3 2.88 is a piggyback letter from an unnamed Ugaritic queen to Urtenu with a second letter from Ilimilku to Urtenu appended. Dewrell's 2025 re-edition therefore supplies a direct Ilimilku → Urtenu communication link.

That matters for network capacity.

It still does not show:
- Ilimilku accessing P332950 / RS 20.123+;
- Urtenu transferring that lexical tablet;
- Attenu accessing that tablet;
- a Dumuzi death-return narrative moving through this network.

## 2. Borrowing-signal test

A source-specific borrowing claim should be stronger than a broad motif match.

High-value signals would include one or more of:
- a distinctive ordered narrative sequence shared at low base rate;
- unusual mechanism-level correspondence;
- translated or calqued formulae;
- a source note, colophon, lexical gloss, or scholastic equation linking the relevant narratives;
- a named carrier tied to the relevant source rather than only to the target tradition;
- directional evidence that discriminates borrowing from regional convergence.

Broad propositions such as:
- a god dies or disappears;
- other gods mourn;
- the god later returns or is alive;
- seasonal or fertility effects follow

are too general by themselves to establish direct textual dependence.

## 3. Dumuzid materials do not form one simple source sequence

The current ON_THEO reconstruction deliberately keeps different Dumuzid witnesses distinct.

Across Sumerian Dumuzid materials, relevant mechanisms include:
- Inana's substitution of Dumuzid;
- galla pursuit and capture;
- escape/transformative assistance;
- Geshtin-ana's sibling role;
- half-year alternation in the underworld.

Mari adds separate and later evidence at different ceilings:
- a damaged/reconstructed death-and-return comparison in A.1146;
- burial evidence;
- temple-entry and reported return material.

These records must not be fused into one imaginary continuous "Dumuzi source text."

## 4. Baal's surviving mechanism is different

The Baal Cycle's controlled death/renewed-life evidence is distributed across distinct physical witnesses.

KTU 1.5:
- Mot conflict context;
- Baal's death/perishing announcement.

KTU 1.6:
- mourning and aftermath;
- Anat/Mot conflict;
- renewed-life recognition;
- restoration of Baal's presence/kingship and rain order.

No current controlled evidence in this lane establishes the Dumuzid-specific package inside the Baal Cycle:
- no substitution of Baal for another deity;
- no galla-like captor sequence;
- no Geshtin-ana-like sibling exchange;
- no half-year alternating underworld arrangement;
- no shared death-return formula;
- no source note naming Dumuzi;
- no scholastic note connecting Dumuzi's narrative to Baal's death/return.

## 6. Current scholarly counter-model

Noga Ayali-Darshan's 2024 monograph, *Dying and Rising Gods: The History of a Mythologem in West Asia of the Second Millennium BCE*, directly challenges the older assumption that Dumuzi was the dominant prototype whose qualities were then transferred to Baal.

Her reconstruction argues from the extant second-millennium evidence that explicit Mesopotamian Dumuzi revival evidence is sparse, while Levantine evidence preserves stronger continuity for the dying-and-rising storm-god mythologem.

This is:

SCHOLARLY_INTERPRETATION / COUNTERMODEL.

It is not automatically adopted as project fact.

But it materially blocks treating east-to-west Dumuzi → Baal direction as the default merely because Dumuzi material is chronologically earlier.

## 8. Search-state boundary

This result does not claim:
- that borrowing was impossible;
- that no lost source ever linked the traditions;
- that oral transmission did not occur;
- that every similarity is independent convergence.

It claims only that the current controlled corpus lacks a source-specific borrowing signal strong enough to discriminate direct Dumuzid → Baal transmission from broader regional circulation or independent adaptation inside a connected cultural zone.
