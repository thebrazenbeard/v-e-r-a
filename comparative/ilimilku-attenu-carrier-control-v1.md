## Purpose

The Dumuzid -> Baal evidence ladder currently marks the named-carrier gate as NOT ESTABLISHED.

This pass asks whether the surviving Ugaritic prosopography supports a stronger but still bounded state.

It does:

A named scribal chain exists around the Baal Cycle.

It does not:

No current evidence ties that named chain to the Dumuzi-specific source P332950 or to a Dumuzi death-return narrative.

## 1. Ilimilku -> Attenu is a direct teacher-student link

KTU 1.6 VI 54-58 directly identifies:
- Ilimilku the Shubbanite;
- as student of Attenu;
- Attenu as prln, currently understood as a diviner/haruspex;
- the wider high-status cultic/court context.

Current EUPT work treats the syntax as clearly connecting prln with Attenu.

KTU 1.179 line 40 independently repeats:
- Ilimilku;
- Shubbanite;
- student of Attenu the prln.

Disposition:

ILIMILKU_ATTENU_TEACHER_STUDENT_CHAIN =
DIRECT_PRIMARY_TEXT_HIGH.

## 2. Attenu's title is Hurrian-derived

Current EUPT analysis connects Ugaritic prln with Hurrian furullinni/purullinni, a professional term interpreted as diviner/haruspex.

This supports:
- a Hurrian technical/professional background in Attenu's title;
- a multilingual/cross-cultural setting around Ilimilku's teacher.

It does not establish:
- that Attenu studied in Babylonia;
- that he taught Mesopotamian Dumuzi literature;
- that he transmitted a Dumuzi death-return narrative.

## 3. The "scribe of Babylon" restoration is not secure

An older reconstruction of damaged KTU 1.179 proposed wording that could be read as:
"[chief of priests and scrib]e of Babel/Babylon."

That reconstruction has sometimes supported a further inference:
Attenu had been trained in Babylonia and was bilingual.

But the restoration is explicitly labeled uncertain in the scholarship that presents it.

Current readable presentations of KTU 1.179 preserve:

line 40:
[spr ilmlk šb]ny lmd atn prln

lines 41-43:
fragmentary wording concerning a recitation/account, teaching, and a document.

Current critical presentations do not supply secure preserved text for:
"scribe of Babylon."

Therefore:

ATTENU_SCRIBE_OF_BABYLON =
OLD_UNCERTAIN_RESTORATION

not:

PRIMARY_TEXT_FACT.

And:

ATTENU_BABYLONIAN_TRAINING =
CONDITIONAL_HYPOTHESIS

not:

ESTABLISHED_CARRIER_EVIDENCE.

## 4. Ilimilku is not confined to one archive

RS 92.2016 / KTU 1.179 was found in the House of Urtenu archive.

Its fragmentary colophon is overwhelmingly attributed to Ilimilku because it preserves:
- Shubbanite identity;
- student of Attenu;
- the same unusual colophon pattern.

This materially shows that Ilimilku's textual activity or textual products extended beyond the House of the High Priest where the Baal Cycle was recovered.

Disposition:

ILIMILKU_CROSS_ARCHIVE_PRESENCE =
SUPPORTED.

This is stronger than:
ILIMILKU_ONLY_HIGH_PRIEST_LIBRARY.

## 5. Urtenu connection does not create a Rapanu connection

P332950, the Dumuzi-bearing lexical tablet, belongs to the House of Rapanu.

KTU 1.179 places Ilimilku in the House of Urtenu archive.

The Baal Cycle belongs to the House of the High Priest.

Thus the current network contains at least three archive contexts:

HOUSE OF RAPANU:
Dumuzi lexical witness.

HOUSE OF URTENU:
Ilimilku-associated mythico-magical text and elite correspondence.

HOUSE OF HIGH PRIEST:
Baal/Kirta/Aqhat literary corpus and Ilimilku colophons.

This establishes:
MULTI_ARCHIVE_SCRIBAL_NETWORK_CAPACITY.

It does not establish:
RAPANU -> URTENU -> ILIMILKU transfer of P332950.

## 6. Ilimilku in elite/diplomatic networks

Current scholarship places Ilimilku in:
- royal service;
- court circles;
- communication involving the queen;
- foreign-affairs or envoy contexts.

Some Akkadian documents from the House of Urtenu may refer to the same Ilimilku.

However, Ugarit contains multiple people named Ilimilku.

Therefore individual identity in non-colophon administrative letters must remain source-specific and caveated.

Control:

ILIMILKU_NAME_IN_ADMINISTRATIVE_TEXT
!=
AUTOMATIC_IDENTITY_WITH_POET_SCRIBE.

The strong carrier evidence should rest first on:
- direct colophons;
- KTU 1.179;
- securely identified scribal context.

## 7. Rapanu / Urtenu direct chain

A targeted V1 search did not recover a direct document showing:
- Rapanu sending RS 20.123+ to Urtenu;
- Urtenu borrowing the Dumuzi tablet;
- Ilimilku accessing Rapanu's lexical archive;
- Attenu accessing the Dumuzi row.

Rapanu and Urtenu were both elite Ugaritic archive owners/official-commercial actors with palace connections.

That is network plausibility.

It is not a documented source-transfer chain.
