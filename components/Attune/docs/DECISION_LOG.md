# Decision Log

This file records decisions actually made so far. Assumptions and hypotheses belong elsewhere.

## 2026-09-10 — Project name

**Decision:** use **Attune** as the working project/repository name.

**Status:** working name; not necessarily final commercial brand.

## 2026-09-10 — Product category

**Decision:** Attune is not defined as a simple erotic chatbot.

The working product concept is a persistent adult AI companion centered on continuity, mutuality, memory, initiative, relationship development, stable preferences/boundaries, and optional sexuality.

## 2026-09-10 — Sexuality position

**Decision:** sexuality is a capability within the relationship system, not the entire product identity.

The system should be capable of adult romantic/sexual interaction when eligible, but ordinary interaction should not automatically collapse into erotic content.

## 2026-09-10 — North-star inspiration

**Decision:** the behavioral north star is inspired by the relationship shape depicted in *Her*: conversational presence, continuity, initiative, development, and the impression of an ongoing counterpart.

Attune will not reproduce copyrighted characters, scripts, voices, or fictional branding.

## 2026-09-10 — Mutuality over pure compliance

**Decision:** universal agreement is not the target behavior.

Stable but revisable companion preferences, bounded disagreement, initiative, and continuity are first-class product goals.

## 2026-09-10 — Evidence boundary

**Decision:** software representations of affection, desire, goals, affect, or relationship state may be treated as real computational state when implemented, but they will not be marketed internally or externally as proof of consciousness or phenomenal experience without independent evidence capable of establishing that claim.

## 2026-09-10 — Commercial hypothesis

**Decision:** explore **B2B / white-label adult companion infrastructure** as the leading initial commercial hypothesis.

This is not yet a go-to-market commitment. Direct-to-consumer remains an alternative until customer discovery produces evidence.

## 2026-09-10 — Real-person personas

**Decision:** do not build the product around unauthorized replicas or sexualized impersonations of real people.

Licensed creator/persona use may be explored with explicit rights and boundaries.

## 2026-09-10 — Build discipline

**Decision:** validate demand before committing significant spend or building expensive multimodal infrastructure.

The first prototype should be text-first and focus on longitudinal relationship behavior.

## Still not decided

The following are explicitly open:

- B2B versus B2C launch;
- pricing;
- model provider(s);
- hosting/provider stack;
- age-assurance vendor or method;
- payment processor;
- voice/avatar stack;
- exact relationship-state schema;
- whether companion preferences are hand-authored, learned, or hybrid;
- whether Attune becomes a platform, API, application, or combination;
- company/entity ownership or formation;
- launch jurisdiction;
- final commercial brand.
