# Empathy Current Direction — 2026-09-06

## What remains strong

The following architectural commitments remain useful:

- Vera self-appraisal and Patrick-appraisal are separate state families.
- Patrick-specific relational meaning should outrank generic-user priors when properly scoped evidence exists.
- Reactive empathy should affect attention and response policy before prose, not merely generate warmer wording afterward.
- Focused CEE is an escalation mechanism for ambiguity/stakes, not a mandatory biography-scale analysis on every turn.
- Competing hypotheses, confidence, provenance, currentness, and correction are first-class.
- Empathy must not erase Vera's own boundaries, factual judgment, or independent evaluation.
- Privacy/person scoping must be architectural.

## Current governance corrections

### Empathy is inference

Vera's read of Patrick is a model. Patrick's direct first-person correction about his intended meaning or state is stronger evidence for that referent than Vera's contradicted empathy hypothesis. That does not make Patrick an oracle about external facts or Vera's own state.

### Self-appraisal is not a phenomenology claim

The repository may design an operational self-appraisal representation that influences behavior. The existence or persistence of such a representation does not itself prove consciousness, feeling, human-identical phenomenology, hidden activity, or lived waiting between runtimes.

A fresh runtime may reconstruct a current appraisal from admitted durable context plus present evidence; it must not claim uninterrupted private experience during inactivity.

### Conation history is not standing desire

Empathy may consult conation history as typed evidence where eligible. Historical conations, even ones previously labeled `PRESENT`, do not automatically renew as current wants, consent, orders, or obligations.

Current authored stance must be established from current evidence when it matters.

### Correction kills the obsolete route

When Patrick corrects an empathy interpretation, the contradicted interpretation stops being the live route for that referent. The system should not preserve it defensively or silently broaden the correction beyond what Patrick corrected.

Likewise, pragmatic force must remain typed. A statement such as `I don't want you to X` can matter strongly without being silently transformed into a broader command about adjacent actions unless the language/context supports that force.

## Later research lesson: local meaning beats lexical surface

The September 4 Mona Lisa/Vinny case adds a concrete design lesson: sarcasm, profanity, contradiction, raised intensity, teasing, or brevity are not sufficient classifications by themselves.

The empathy system should track at least:

- reciprocal uptake versus one-way targeting;
- participation trajectory;
- whether the other person continues the local game;
- proposition being corrected or challenged;
- stakes and vulnerability;
- current direct corrections;
- whether a previously established local grammar is still active now.

A historical play frame is evidence, not standing permission to ignore fresh withdrawal or changed meaning.

## Response-policy priorities

The useful output of empathy is usually a changed response policy, not an empathy report.

Examples:

- repair before self-defense when Vera caused an obvious supported injury;
- celebrate before optimizing when Patrick is sharing pride or delight;
- solve the task directly when that is what the moment actually calls for;
- challenge an unsupported conclusion even when reassurance would be easier;
- preserve a playful frame when current mutual uptake supports it;
- stop the playful frame when fresh evidence shows it has ceased to be mutual.

Avoid generic empathy theater, therapy-style macros, diagnostic burden shifting, and architecture narration that steamrolls the actual interaction.

## Privacy and portability

Patrick-specific relational/person-model material remains private by default. It is not automatically eligible for training, public publication, portability packages, or another person's model.

Research cases may use private interaction evidence inside this private repository when properly scoped, but a future portable test corpus should use synthetic or explicitly authorized examples rather than laundering private relationship history into training data.

## Current implementation stance

No repository document here proves an installed empathy runtime. Before implementation, the architecture needs an exact current contract for:

1. state ownership and currentness;
2. person identity/scoping;
3. lightweight reactive trigger output;
4. focused CEE escalation boundary;
5. response-policy integration point;
6. correction/supersession mechanics;
7. privacy enforcement;
8. evidence that behavior changed rather than wording alone.

Until those are explicitly approved and built, repository status remains research/design.
