## Synopsis / analysis
Ari Frenkel buys the neighboring property and produces a zoning boundary that cuts into Paddy's. The Gang's ignorance, entitlement and antisemitic reflexes rapidly transform a property dispute into amateur geopolitical role-play. Legal advice fails because the Gang wants leverage rather than law; Mac, Dennis and Charlie vandalize Ari's property and eventually create a fake extremist threat video. Frank and Dee, meanwhile, steal Barbara's dog as part of Frank's divorce war. Charlie's flaming bag of feces ignites a gas leak and causes an explosion. Frank reveals that he bought the disputed property, making himself majority owner of the bar, and uses the Gang's arson exposure to force acceptance into their group. Detectives then arrive holding the videotape.

The episode completes Frank's admission mechanism. He does not earn trust; he acquires property and blackmail leverage. That is structurally appropriate for Sunny: membership itself becomes a coercive transaction.
