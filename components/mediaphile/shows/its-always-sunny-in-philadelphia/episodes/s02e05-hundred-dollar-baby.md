## Synopsis / analysis
After the men flee a mugger and leave Dee behind, she pursues boxing/self-defense under Frank. Mac and Dennis, impressed by an underground fight's betting market, decide Charlie's tolerance for punishment makes him their ideal fighter and train him by getting drunk and beating him. At Frank's old gym, Dee becomes entangled in Frank's rivalry with Bobby Thunderson and Bobby's daughter Brianna. Dee is offered “performance-enhancing supplements”; Charlie steals them; both become more aggressive and unstable. Their own confrontation ends with an assault and arrest, removing Charlie from the planned fight. Mac substitutes and loses; Dennis loses his betting money; Frank sucker-punches Bobby, whose fall injures Brianna.

The episode mocks inspirational boxing narratives by replacing discipline with intoxication, exploitation and pharmacological shortcuts. Charlie's friends value his durability because it makes him economically exploitable; Dee's desire not to be helpless after the mugging gets diverted into Frank's old grievance.
