## Synopsis / analysis
A MySpace contact named Bruce Mathis tells Dee he is her and Dennis's biological father. Barbara confirms it immediately, humiliating Frank and destabilizing Dennis and Dee's family story. Bruce's identity as a philanthropic, emotionally generous man initially appeals to them until spending time with dying children exposes how little they value altruism without personal reward. In parallel, Mac and Charlie visit Mac's imprisoned father Luther and try to impress him. Frank recruits them to attack Bruce, but they cannot complete the job. Frank later reconnects with an old lover—Bonnie Kelly—and Charlie realizes that Frank may be his biological father.

The finale deliberately scrambles paternity while clarifying chosen affinity. Bruce may be the Reynolds twins' biological father but is temperamentally alien to them; Frank may not be their biological father but has already become the person whose appetites and schemes fit the Gang. Charlie's possible link to Frank makes the emerging Charlie/Frank household both more intimate and more uncertain.
