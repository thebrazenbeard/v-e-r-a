# S03E02 — The Gang Gets Invincible

Sources: Springfield transcript `s03e02`; Wikipedia season 3 table.

The Eagles' open tryouts trigger a Gang-wide sports fantasy. Mac and Dennis compete as would-be players; Dee disguises herself as a man and proves naturally gifted as a kicker; Frank and Charlie tailgate, use LSD, and encounter the extended McPoyle clan. Charlie revives Green Man, his old unofficial high-school mascot persona. The tryout degrades through injury, intoxication and rivalry: Mac is crushed by Doyle McPoyle, Dennis is knocked out by a football, Dee breaks her foot after revealing herself, and Frank shoots Doyle in the leg.

The comedy attacks aspirational sports-movie logic. Everyone wants the triumph montage without discipline, and Dee—briefly the most competent athlete—still destroys her opportunity through showmanship and the surrounding chaos. First major Green Man appearance.
