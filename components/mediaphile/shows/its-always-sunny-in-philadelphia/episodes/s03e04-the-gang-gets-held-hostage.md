The McPoyles seize Paddy's and hold Mac, Dennis and Dee hostage while Frank is already crawling through the ventilation system searching for a will Charlie hid. Dee begins adapting to the captors and displaying Stockholm-syndrome behavior; Dennis tries sexual manipulation on Margaret McPoyle; the captives are forced to damage their own bar. Charlie escapes into the vents and joins Frank. On the roof, the McPoyles' supposed execution threat collapses when their guns are revealed as fake.

The episode literalizes Paddy's as a trap and turns every survival response into character pathology: Dee seeks captor approval, Dennis weaponizes desirability, Frank treats vents as normal territory, and Charlie is uniquely suited to navigate the building's filthy infrastructure. The McPoyles shift from nuisance antagonists into a family-scale threat.
