Dee discovers that Ingrid “Fatty Magoo” Nelson, her mocked high-school friend, has become a successful fashion entrepreneur. Dee's jealousy becomes a desperate attempt to prove she is the true designer, while Dennis assumes he can model and Mac/Charlie become tangled in garment production. Frank revives his old sweatshop instincts and coaches Mac in exploitative labor practices.

The episode makes high-school hierarchy reversible but not escapable: Ingrid moved on and built something; Dee still organizes self-worth around the old comparison. Frank's casual nostalgia for sweatshop management expands his pre-Gang history and shows why he integrates so easily into any scheme that turns people into inputs. The title itself preserves the cruel high-school nicknames as the emotional frame Dee cannot outgrow.
