A corporate restaurant chain offers to buy Paddy's. The men fantasize about wealth and try to impress the representative, while Dee learns she will not share in the sale and quits. She pressures the Waitress into getting her a job at a chain restaurant, where scripted enthusiasm and managerial control make Dee confront what corporate employment actually demands. The proposed Paddy's sale also exposes how little the Gang understands the bar's economics or their own attachment to it.

The episode distinguishes wanting money from wanting to relinquish Paddy's. Their bar is objectively failing, yet it is also the physical system that lets them avoid ordinary accountability. “Selling out” threatens to convert their chaotic sovereignty into jobs, and that turns out to be less attractive than the windfall fantasy.
