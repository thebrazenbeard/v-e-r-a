# S03E14 — Bums: Making a Mess All Over the City

Sources: Springfield `s03e14`; Wikipedia season 3.

Mac and Dee appoint themselves vigilantes against homelessness. Frank and Dennis buy a junkyard police car, dress as police and discover that impersonated authority gives them permission to bully strangers. Charlie, obsessed with *Serpico*, takes the opposite role and tries to expose corruption.

Every faction wants policing as theater. Mac and Dee want righteous violence without civic responsibility; Frank and Dennis want immunity and deference; Charlie wants the lone-honest-cop narrative. The episode turns public disorder into a costume box for people more interested in inhabiting authority archetypes than solving anything. It also extends the Gang's recurring self-appointed law-enforcement behavior from “Gun Fever” into explicit impersonation.
