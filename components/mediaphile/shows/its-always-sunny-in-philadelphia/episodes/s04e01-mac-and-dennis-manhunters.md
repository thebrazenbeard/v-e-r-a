Mac and Dennis decide hunting animals is insufficiently challenging and begin hunting Cricket as human prey. Charlie and Dee, meanwhile, become convinced Frank fed them human flesh and discover an escalating craving they interpret as cannibalism. Frank eventually reveals the meat was raccoon. Both plots run on self-diagnosis by fantasy: Mac/Dennis want to be predators, while Charlie/Dee turn an ordinary appetite into evidence of monstrous transformation. Cricket's degradation continues as the Gang literally treats him as game.
