# S04E02 — The Gang Solves the Gas Crisis

Sources: Springfield S04E02 transcript; Wikipedia season 4.

The Gang buys gasoline to hoard and resell after prices rise, storing it in garbage cans and trying door-to-door sales. Their self-assigned roles—brains, looks, wildcard—become more important than understanding fuel markets or basic safety. Frank and Dee separately try to frame Bruce Mathis as a terrorist and instead terrorize an innocent stranger. Charlie's “wildcard” behavior culminates in cutting the brakes and leaping from the van. The episode codifies the Gang's habit of assigning team archetypes as if naming competence creates it, while every response to a large social problem remains a tiny personal scam.
