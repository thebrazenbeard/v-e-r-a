# S04E08 — Paddy's Pub: The Worst Bar in Philadelphia

`TEXT_INGESTED` — metadata + released transcript; audiovisual false. Released 2008-10-16.

Sources: Springfield S04E08 transcript (site label varies to “Worst Bar in America”); Wikipedia season 4 preserves released title.

A newspaper critic calls Paddy's the worst bar in Philadelphia. The Gang attempts to force a better review by kidnapping the critic, then accidentally expands the hostage problem to his neighbor. What begins as reputation management becomes imprisonment because nobody can tolerate letting an outside observer's judgment stand. The critic's complaint is essentially validated in real time: the Gang responds to being called dangerous and incompetent by becoming more dangerous and incompetent.
