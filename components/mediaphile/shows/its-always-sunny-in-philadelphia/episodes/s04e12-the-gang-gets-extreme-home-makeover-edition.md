# S04E12 — The Gang Gets Extreme: Home Makeover Edition

Sources: Springfield S04E12 transcript; Wikipedia season 4.

Inspired by makeover television and convinced generosity will earn them karmic rewards, the Gang selects the Juarez family for an unsolicited home transformation. They abduct and terrorize the family, destroy the house, and rationalize every violation as benevolence because the end product is supposed to be inspirational. Their attempt at altruism is therefore structurally indistinguishable from a home invasion. The episode is a brutal example of intention being treated by the Gang as moral immunity.
