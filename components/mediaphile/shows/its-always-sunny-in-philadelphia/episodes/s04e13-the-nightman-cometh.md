# S04E13 — The Nightman Cometh

Sources: Springfield S04E13 transcript; Wikipedia season 4.

Charlie writes and directs a musical built from his Night Man mythology and casts the Gang. Rehearsals expose incompatible egos: Dennis insists on erotic presentation, Mac embraces the Night Man role, Dee wants artistic respect, and Frank plays the Troll. The apparent artistic project is ultimately Charlie's elaborate marriage proposal to the Waitress. She rejects him publicly.

The episode transforms the trauma-coded material introduced in S03E09 into Charlie's most ambitious authored work. It demonstrates real musical/theatrical capability while also showing that his art remains subordinated to the Waitress obsession. The Gang can produce something surprisingly coherent only when Charlie imposes enough structure to contain them—and even then the true objective is personal compulsion.
