The Gang tries to leave Philadelphia for a road trip to the Grand Canyon and repeatedly fails to get meaningfully out of the region. Poor preparation, stolen goods, drinking, territorial arguments and Charlie's lack of travel experience turn movement itself into the plot. The episode establishes how geographically and psychologically enclosed the Gang is: an ordinary trip becomes nearly impossible because they cannot coordinate long enough to leave their familiar system.
