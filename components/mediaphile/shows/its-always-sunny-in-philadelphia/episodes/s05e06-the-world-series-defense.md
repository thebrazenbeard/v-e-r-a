In court over parking tickets accumulated during the Phillies' 2008 World Series run, the Gang reconstructs its increasingly absurd attempts to attend the game. Charlie's Green Man persona, Mac's Chase Utley fixation and the Gang's civic/sports identity converge. Their “defense” is really a celebration of entitlement: championship euphoria is treated as suspension of normal rules. The framing also shows their reflex to turn a legal proceeding into performance rather than evidence.
