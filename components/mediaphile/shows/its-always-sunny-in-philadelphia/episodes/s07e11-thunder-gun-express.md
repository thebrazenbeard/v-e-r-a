The Gang races across Philadelphia to reach a screening of *Thunder Gun Express*, splitting into incompatible transportation strategies. Their reverence for the film's masculine mythology—especially the promise that the hero “hangs dong”—turns attendance into a quest. The city itself becomes an obstacle course revealing who can improvise, manipulate strangers and abandon one another fastest. The Gang loves cinematic heroism most when it excuses selfishness.
