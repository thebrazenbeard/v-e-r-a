After learning Paddy's has again been ignored by a local bar awards system, the Gang reverse-engineers the traits of decorated competitors: brightness, friendliness, accessible drinks and an upbeat house style. Their imitation collapses because they cannot suppress their contempt for customers or one another. Charlie's increasingly hostile original song becomes the bluntest expression of that resentment. The episode functions simultaneously as a bar-industry plot and a meta-comedy about *Sunny* existing outside conventional approval systems.
