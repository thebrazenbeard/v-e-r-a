Preparing for a Boyz II Men singing competition, the Gang responds to a flu scare by sealing themselves inside Paddy's. Their quarantine rapidly becomes coercive isolation, with Dennis taking command and interpreting every symptom through fear. The final explanation reframes much of the episode: several of the Gang's apparent illness symptoms are alcohol withdrawal. The joke is not merely that they misdiagnose themselves; their baseline substance dependence is so normalized that forced sobriety feels like an external disease.
