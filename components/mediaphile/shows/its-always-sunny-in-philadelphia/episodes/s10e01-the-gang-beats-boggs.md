On a cross-country flight, the Gang attempts to break the legendary beer total attributed to Wade Boggs. Mac appoints himself commissioner, Frank turns the contest into gambling, Dee refuses the men's attempt to exclude her, and Charlie persists long after ordinary judgment has disappeared. The episode turns a sports legend into one of the Gang's self-made rituals: rules, records and competition matter chiefly because they create a hierarchy the characters can fight over.
