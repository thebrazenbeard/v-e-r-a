A zoo visit is coordinated largely through group texts, private messages, emojis and mistaken recipients. The Gang's already poor pragmatic communication becomes worse when tone, audience and referent have to be inferred from short digital messages. Alliances form around perceived slights that often exist only because the wrong person received or interpreted a text. The episode is a useful communication artifact: channel constraints amplify existing ambiguity rather than create it from nothing.
