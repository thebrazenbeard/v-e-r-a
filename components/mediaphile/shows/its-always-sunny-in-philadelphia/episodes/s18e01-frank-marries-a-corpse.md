Season 17's unusually sincere Frank/Sam ending carries directly into their Paddy's wedding. Frank develops cold feet while Dee attempts to scare Sam away with testimony about the Gang, but the decisive disaster is physical: after Sam and Frank's exposure to contaminated creek water, a flesh-eating infection kills Sam before the ceremony. Dennis and Dee, already treating Sam's fortune as their future inheritance, conceal her death and physically manipulate her corpse through the wedding so the marriage will appear completed. The scheme lasts until Frank kisses Sam and her head comes off. At the same time Charlie is already mourning Bonnie, carrying his mother's ashes into the wedding. The premiere therefore destroys Frank's new relationship and establishes Bonnie's death while preserving the Gang's oldest rule: money can convert another person's catastrophe into an administrative problem to solve.
