Dennis and Dee continue trying to convert Sam's death into wealth, chasing paperwork, legal transfer and eventually access to her fingerprints while imagining themselves already above the working-class people they still need. In parallel, Charlie and Frank build an extravagant joint memorial for Bonnie and Sam around Philadelphia's Mummers culture; the escalating funeral budget even puts Bonnie's house at risk. The two plots collide when Frank places Sam's remains in a cannon and fires them as part of the send-off, destroying Dennis and Dee's hoped-for access to her thumbs and therefore their inheritance scheme. The episode turns grief and greed into competing uses for the same corpse while giving Frank and Charlie's memorial planning a surprising core of genuine affection for the two dead women.
