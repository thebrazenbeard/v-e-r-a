## Open chronology problems

### Birth year

Matthew's Herodian setting and Luke's Quirinius census reference are difficult to place in one straightforward timeline. Do not manufacture precision by harmonization.

### Panthera

The inscription shows the name in the relevant era; Celsus shows the paternity claim by the late second century. The gap between those facts is exactly where unsupported internet claims tend to appear.

### Gospel / noncanonical dates

Use ranges and record scholarly disagreement. A final text's date does not necessarily date every tradition inside it.

### Qur'anic Jesus

Always tag first-century claimed events separately from seventh-century Qur'anic source date.
