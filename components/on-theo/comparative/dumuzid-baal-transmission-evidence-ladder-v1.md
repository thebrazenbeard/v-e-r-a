## Purpose

The Dumuzid/Baal lane now contains enough source-level work that a prose phrase such as "possible influence" is no longer precise enough.

This packet converts the accumulated research into a gate matrix.

The question is not:

"Are Dumuzid and Baal similar?"

The question is:

Which requirements for historical transmission are currently supported, unresolved, contradicted, or superseded?

## 1. Gate vocabulary

PASS:
required proposition is positively supported at the relevant evidence level.

PARTIAL:
some relevant evidence exists, but it does not satisfy the full transmission requirement.

OPEN:
the proposition remains materially unresolved.

NOT_ESTABLISHED:
the project has looked for the required evidence and has not established it.

SUPERSEDED:
an earlier project statement is replaced by later stronger/corrective evidence.

NONE_ESTABLISHED:
no direct transmission edge is justified.

These labels do not imply historical impossibility.

## 2. Gate 1 — independent source reconstruction

### Dumuzid side

PASS WITH CONTROLS.

The project has directly reconstructed:
- Inana's Descent half-year alternation;
- Dumuzid and Geshtin-ana substitution/galla capture;
- Dumuzid's Dream death/capture/lament material;

Major caution:

Gate result:
PASS.

## 3. Gate 2 — chronology

Old Babylonian Dumuzid traditions and eighteenth-century Mari Dumuzi material predate the surviving Late Bronze Age Baal Cycle tablets.

This satisfies chronological possibility.

It does not establish dependence.

Gate result:
PASS.

## 4. Gate 3 — regional contact

Mari supplies an eighteenth-century West Asian Dumuzi contact field:
- death/return comparison in A.1146 under reconstruction controls;
- Dumuzi burial in A.4540;
- temple-entry movement in A.512;
- reported/administrative temple-entry evidence in M.13167;
- additional publication and return-rite evidence with lower ceilings.

Gate result:
PASS FOR REGIONAL CONTACT FIELD.

## 5. Gate 4 — Mesopotamian literary contact at Ugarit

Physical Akkadian Gilgamesh tablets and cuneiform lexical/school texts were excavated at Ugarit.

Thus Mesopotamian literary and scholastic material was materially present at the target city.

Gate result:
PASS.

## 6. Gate 5 — Dumuzi-specific exposure at Ugarit

RS 20.123+ / P332950 directly preserves:
- Dumuzi in the Mesopotamian column;
- Hurrian du-mu-zi;
- lost Ugaritic-equivalent field.

This supersedes the earlier claim that no Dumuzi-specific source had been identified at Ugarit.

Gate result:
PASS FOR DUMUZI-SPECIFIC SCHOLASTIC EXPOSURE.

Important scope:
this is a lexical/scholastic source, not a Dumuzi narrative.

## 7. Gate 6 — institutional carrier environment

P332950 belongs to the House of Rapanu scribal archive/training environment.

The Baal Cycle belongs to the separate House of the High Priest corpus.

Both sit inside one multilingual learned city.

This materially grounds:
UGARITIC_SCRIBAL_NETWORK
as a plausible carrier class.

But the two sources are not from one archive.

Gate result:
PASS FOR INSTITUTIONAL CAPACITY.

## 8. Gate 7 — named carrier

Ilimilku is directly named as scribe on KTU 1.6 and directly attested on KTU 1.4.

No evidence currently shows that:
- Ilimilku accessed RS 20.123+;
- Ilimilku read another Dumuzi source;
- Rapanu's archive supplied material to the House of the High Priest;
- a named intermediary carried a Dumuzi death/return text to Ilimilku.

Gate result:
NOT_ESTABLISHED.

This is one of the decisive missing links.

## 9. Gate 8 — Dumuzi narrative at Ugarit

No controlled Dumuzi death/return narrative has been identified at Ugarit.

The positive Dumuzi witness is scholastic.

Targeted alphabetic-Ugaritic searches found no controlled Dumuzi/Tammuz:
- deity-name occurrence;
- ritual recipient;
- offering-list entry;
- mythological character

independent of the syllabic lexical witness.

This is bounded search-state evidence.

Gate result:
NOT_ESTABLISHED.

Historical absence is not proven.

## 10. Gate 9 — local Dumuzi equivalence

P332950's Ugaritic-equivalent field beside Dumuzi is lost.

Targeted parallel search did not recover it.

Therefore:
- Dumuzi = Baal is not established;
- Dumuzi = any other specific Ugaritic deity is not established.

Gate result:
OPEN / LOST DATUM.

## 11. Gate 10 — scholastic equation semantics

The same P332950 tablet preserves:
- Utu = Shimigi = Shapshu;
- Imzuanna = Teshub = Baal.

The Imzuanna/Teshub/Baal row is a hostile control showing that god-list equation can reflect learned/sign-based scribal logic and does not automatically equal full theological identity.

Thus even a future recovered Dumuzi local equivalent would first establish:
SCHOLASTIC_EQUIVALENCE.

It would not automatically establish:
NARRATIVE_DEPENDENCE.

Gate result:
PASS AS METHOD CONTROL.

## 12. Gate 11 — proposition-level semantic match

High-level similarities are real:
- divine death/disappearance;
- lament/divine response;
- renewed presence;
- seasonal interpretations.

But important mechanisms differ.

Dumuzid:
- substitution;
- galla captors;
- escape;
- sister relation;
- explicit half-year alternation.

Baal:
- Mot conflict;
- death/perishing language;
- Anat/El response;
- renewed-life recognition;
- rain/kingship framework.

Gate result:
PARTIAL STRUCTURAL MATCH.

Not:
IDENTICAL NARRATIVE ARCHITECTURE.

## 13. Gate 12 — lexical/formula dependence

The surviving formulations do not establish a shared lexical death-return formula.

A.1146:
- death/harm verb exact lemma disputed;
- common killing translation reconstruction-dependent;
- iterative taru return remains controlled.

Baal:
- mt / hlq death-perish wording;
- hy / existence/alive wording.

No direct translation-calque evidence has been established.

Gate result:
NOT_ESTABLISHED.

## 14. Gate 13 — source-specific borrowing signal

No current evidence identifies:
- a copied Dumuzid line in the Baal Cycle;
- a distinctive Dumuzid formula translated into Ugaritic;
- a uniquely Dumuzid ritual sequence reproduced by Baal;
- a tablet colophon naming a Mesopotamian source;
- a scholastic note connecting Dumuzi with Baal's death-return story.

Gate result:
NOT_ESTABLISHED.

## 15. Gate 14 — directionality

Chronology allows east-to-west influence.

Mari and Ugarit demonstrate real contact fields.

But current scholarship also challenges the old assumption that Dumuzi was necessarily the dominant prototype for West Asian dying-and-rising traditions.

Possible models remain:
- Mesopotamian influence westward;
- wider shared West Asian mythologem;
- western influence on some Dumuzi return traditions;
- bidirectional interaction;
- independent development inside a connected cultural zone.

Gate result:
UNRESOLVED.

## 16. Gate 15 — direct transmission

A direct Dumuzid -> Baal transmission claim would require, at minimum:
- chronology: PASS;
- contact: PASS;
- target exposure: PASS;
- relevant source exposure: not just divine-name exposure;
- identifiable carrier or chain;
- proposition-level dependency stronger than generic motif;
- directional evidence;
- source-specific borrowing signal.

The current matrix lacks several required gates.

Therefore:

DIRECT_DUMUZID_TO_BAAL_TRANSMISSION =
NONE_ESTABLISHED.

## 17. Positive conclusion that *is* justified

The evidence now supports a stronger bounded statement than the project could support at the beginning:

Ugarit was not isolated from Mesopotamian religious/literary scholarship.
Dumuzi specifically was present in Ugarit's learned lexical culture.
Baal was also embedded in the same multilingual god-list system.
The city therefore had a materially grounded environment in which cross-cultural religious knowledge could circulate.

That makes influence historically plausible.

It does not identify the content, direction, carrier, or dependency required for a direct lineage claim.
