## Purpose

PR #49 established that the three Mari Dumuzi records used in the transmission test have unequal source control.

This successor addresses two remaining weaknesses:

1. resolve the bibliographic identity of MARI 5 (1987) no. 14;
2. separate direct Dumuzi temple-entry movement evidence from stronger interpretations such as "return from death" or "resurrection."

Core rule:

TEMPLE_ENTRY_RITUAL != BODILY_RESURRECTION

and:

PUBLICATION_IDENTITY != LINE_LEVEL_TRANSLITERATION

## 2. Charpin's 1987 publication context

CDLI and current bibliographic databases identify Charpin's MARI 5 article as:

"Nouveaux documents du bureau de l'huile (suite),"
pp. 597-599.

A modern CNRS bibliography of published Mari texts reports that this article published fourteen texts for the first time.

This places no. 14 inside an oil-office / administrative documentation context rather than a literary mythological composition.

Control:

MARI5_NO14 = ADMINISTRATIVE/CULTIC_RECORD_CONTEXT

not:

MARI5_NO14 = MYTHOLOGICAL_NARRATIVE.

## 3. A.512 gives direct temple-entry wording

A.512, a letter of Itur-Asdu from Mari, preserves a direct Dumuzi temple-entry statement.

Published transliteration quoted in Shigeo Yamada's study:

dDUMU.ZI
ana E Annunitim
sha Mari
usherib

Translation:

"I had Dumuzi enter the temple of Annunitum of Mari."

The surrounding letter also refers to:
- the pudum obligation/rotation;
- the temple of Annunitum of Sehrum;
- sacrifice by the queen.

Disposition:

A512_DUMUZI_TEMPLE_ENTRY =
DIRECT_TEXTUAL_SUPPORT.

This is materially stronger than a modern summary saying simply "Dumuzi returned."

## 4. What A.512 does not say

The direct A.512 wording does not itself say:
- Dumuzi rose from the dead;
- Dumuzi emerged from the netherworld;
- the entry was a resurrection;
- a cult statue specifically was carried, unless separately reconstructed from cultic practice;
- the rite copied a Ugaritic or other West Semitic death-return tradition.

The safest source-level proposition is movement/presentation:

DUMUZI_ENTERED_ANNUNITUM_TEMPLE.

Any interpretation of what physical object, image, divine presence, or ritual mechanism "entered" is a secondary historical reconstruction.

## 5. M.13167 gives a second temple-entry context

A later scholarly study of the goddess Belet-ekallim reports M.13167 as an oil-ration record for Dumuzi:

"when entering the temple of Belet-ekallim."

The study states:
- a transcription is published by Duponchel in Gs Barrelet (1997), p. 222;
- the text is dated 10/x/ZL 2.

This is important because it places Dumuzi temple-entry language in an administrative ration context rather than only in A.512's letter.

Disposition:

M13167_DUMUZI_BELET_EKALLIM_ENTRY =
PUBLISHED_TRANSCRIPTION_REPORTED /
DIRECT_TRANSCRIPTION_NOT_YET_COLLATION-CHECKED_IN_THIS_PASS.

Evidence ceiling:
stronger than generic synthesis;
weaker than direct independent readback of Duponchel's transcription.

## 6. M.15090 is a parallel but remains publication-reference-only

The same Belet-ekallim study reports that Charpin, MARI 5 (1987), p. 599, mentions an unpublished parallel text:

M.15090
dated 6/x/ZL 5

with the same formula concerning Dumuzi entering Belet-ekallim.

Because M.15090 is described there as unpublished:

M15090_CURRENT_V1 =
UNPUBLISHED_PARALLEL_REPORTED_BY_SCHOLARSHIP.

It must not be given the same line-level confidence as A.512.

## 7. Month X / winter interpretation

M.13167 is dated month X in the Mari calendar.

Mari calendar scholarship identifies month X as Belet-biri and places it in the winter portion of the calendar.

This makes a winter "return/entry" interpretation historically plausible.

But:

MONTH_X_DATE + TEMPLE_ENTRY
!=
PRIMARY_TEXT_STATEMENT "DUMUZI RESURRECTED IN WINTER."

The calendar correlation is historical reconstruction.

The Mari Dumuzi evidence now supports several distinct source-level propositions:

RESURRECTION.

## 9. Cult-image hypothesis

A natural historical reconstruction is that "Dumuzi entered the temple" refers to movement of:
- a cult image;
- a divine symbol;
- a ritual representation;
- or a divine-presence concept instantiated through ritual movement.

That can explain how a deity "enters" a temple administratively.

But the source-level grammar does not itself specify the ontology of the moving entity.

Therefore:

CULT_IMAGE_MOVEMENT =
PLAUSIBLE_HISTORICAL_RECONSTRUCTION

not:

EXPLICIT_TEXTUAL_FACT.

## 10. Effect on the Mari contact-field model

The contact-field claim becomes stronger in one respect and narrower in another.

Stronger:
Mari now has multiple, differentiated records concerning:
- Dumuzi death/return language;
- burial;
- temple-entry movement;
- administrative provisioning around Dumuzi cult.

Narrower:
none of these records by themselves establish:
- bodily resurrection;
- direct influence on Baal;
- one standardized annual dying/rising ritual.

Recommended formulation:

Old Babylonian Mari preserves a materially attested cultic field in which Dumuzi could be buried, invoked through death/return language, and represented as entering temples in calendrically situated ritual contexts.
