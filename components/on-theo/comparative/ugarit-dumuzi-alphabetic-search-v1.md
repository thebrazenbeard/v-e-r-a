Ugarit's alphabetic corpus includes:

- sacrificial texts;

- incantations;
- mythological texts.

A Dumuzi/Tammuz occurrence in one of those genres would materially strengthen local reception.

No such occurrence was established in this V1 pass.

## 5. No speculative cognate promotion

No consonantal form encountered in the targeted search was promoted merely because it could be made to resemble:
- Dumuzi;
- Tammuz;
- dmz;
- tmz.

A defensible cognate claim requires:
- philological derivation;
- phonological compatibility;
- source context;
- semantic fit;
- scholarly support.

Control:

LOOKALIKE_CONSONANTS != COGNATE.

## 6. Divine-name index has its own limits

The AEMW/Ugarit divine-name index is a strong current search surface, but:
- digital corpora can be incomplete;
- damaged names may be unindexed;
- uncertain readings may evade normalized search;
- unpublished or newly joined fragments may change the result;
- a deity can be referenced indirectly or by title rather than preserved name.

Therefore the non-hit is bounded to:
CURRENT_SEARCHABLE_INDEX_AND_QUERIES.
