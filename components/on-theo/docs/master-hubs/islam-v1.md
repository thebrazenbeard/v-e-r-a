## Core chronology rule

The Qur'an belongs to the seventh century CE.

Therefore it is not a first-century source for the historical Yeshua.

Its historical value for Yeshua/Jesus research is instead:
- reception;
- reinterpretation;
- late-antique tradition history;
- preservation or reframing of Jewish/Christian motifs.
