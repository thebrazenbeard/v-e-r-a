# Commonality matrix

This matrix compares conceptual structures, not truth claims. Cells compress internally diverse traditions and should link back to branch research before being used as evidence.

| Theme | Jewish / Second Temple frame | Christian development | Qur'anic / Islamic frame | Cross-tradition structure | Systems analogy |
|---|---|---|---|---|---|
| Ultimate authority | One God of Israel; creator/covenant sovereign | Same God reinterpreted through Father/Son/Spirit and Christology | Radical divine unity; creator without divine offspring | reality has a highest-order authority | root/admin authority |
| Creation | God creates/orders world; speech/word imagery in scripture | creation through God; later Logos/Christ participates in creation | creation by sovereign command; "Be" | reality depends on authority beyond ordinary creature agency | initialization/state creation |
| Human distinctiveness | image of God, covenant, Torah, wisdom, moral responsibility | inherited Jewish anthropology plus Spirit/new-creation themes | Adam receives knowledge; fitrah; vicegerency/stewardship traditions | humans are not merely passive matter; they have role/responsibility | instantiated agent architecture |

## Strong convergences

At high abstraction, all three traditions can support a pattern like:

`ultimate authority -> created order -> morally responsible humans -> revelation/guidance -> contested human response -> death -> divine action beyond death -> judgment/restoration/enduring order`

That common architecture is historically unsurprising because Christianity emerges from Judaism and Islam emerges later in a Late Antique environment saturated with Jewish and Christian traditions.

It is therefore **not** evidence by itself for an independent common external source.

## High-value divergences

The disagreements are at least as informative as the overlap:

- Is Jesus merely human, uniquely authorized, divine, or both human and divine?
- Did crucifixion occur as the Christian narrative claims?
- Does Jesus's death atone for sin?
- Is divine sonship legitimate language?
- Is God internally triune or absolutely non-triune?
- What remains binding from Torah?
- Which revelation/community correctly interprets earlier revelation?

Any GOD/simulation synthesis that makes these differences disappear is too lossy to be useful.
