# Buddhism — Diachronic Overview V1

Status: RESEARCH_PACKET / REVIEW_REQUIRED

## 1. Method

This packet does not define one timeless "Buddhist doctrine."

It distinguishes:
- early discourse traditions;
- monastic law;
- early schools;
- Aśokan and post-Aśokan material evidence;
- Abhidharma developments;
- Mahāyāna textual traditions;
- later Indian Buddhist philosophy;
- East Asian and Tibetan developments.

Core rule:

EARLY_PARALLEL_ATTESTATION
!=
LATER_SCHOOL_SYSTEMATIZATION.

## 2. Early textual transmission

SuttaCentral's corpus architecture is useful because it places Pali Nikāya texts beside parallels preserved in Chinese Āgamas, Sanskrit fragments, Tibetan material, and other Indic witnesses.

This permits proposition-level comparison across transmission lineages.

Source:
https://suttacentral.net/introduction

Research rule:

PALI_CANON
!=
SOLE_DIRECT_TRANSCRIPT_OF_EARLIEST_BUDDHISM.

A proposition repeated across independent early transmission lines is methodologically stronger than one surviving in only a late or isolated witness.

## 3. Canon structure

The Theravāda Tipiṭaka is traditionally organized as:
- Vinaya Piṭaka;
- Sutta Piṭaka;
- Abhidhamma Piṭaka.

The large discourse collections include:
- Dīgha Nikāya;
- Majjhima Nikāya;
- Saṃyutta Nikāya;
- Aṅguttara Nikāya;
- Khuddaka Nikāya.

The collections do not all have the same compositional history.

Do not infer:
CANONICAL_NEIGHBORHOOD = SAME_DATE.

## 4. Early doctrinal proposition set

High-priority reconstruction topics:
- dukkha;
- dependent arising / paṭicca-samuppāda;
- impermanence / anicca;
- non-self / anattā;
- karma;
- rebirth;
- nirvāṇa;
- jhāna;
- monastic discipline;
- path models.

Each claim should record:
- Pali locus;
- Chinese parallel if any;
- Sanskrit/Gāndhārī parallel if any;
- whether vocabulary is shared or translation-dependent;
- likely textual layer;
- later doctrinal reinterpretation.

## 5. Non-self is not a soul-substitute doctrine

Early Buddhist non-self arguments must be reconstructed from source passages rather than reduced to:
"there is no person."

A better project question is:
which candidate phenomena are denied permanent, controlling, or self-identical status, and what causal continuity remains?

Do not silently replace:
anattā
with:
modern materialism
or:
annihilationism.

## 6. Dependent arising

Dependent arising is central but not textually uniform in presentation.

The project should distinguish:
- short causal formulas;
- twelve-link formulations;
- psychological/phenomenological readings;
- rebirth-cycle readings;
- later Madhyamaka use.

Guard:

PRATITYASAMUTPADA
!=
ONE_SINGLE_FIXED_METAPHYSICAL_THEORY.

## 7. Nirvāṇa

Do not normalize nirvāṇa as:
- heaven;
- nonexistence;
- mystical union;
- psychological calm

without source-specific control.

Research must distinguish:
- descriptions of cessation;
- liberation while alive;
- post-mortem questions;
- later school metaphysics.

## 8. Aśokan and material evidence

The third-century-BCE Aśokan period is a major independent historical anchor for Buddhist institutional expansion.

Material evidence includes:
- inscriptions;
- pillars;
- stupas;
- patronage contexts.

Later textual legends about Aśoka must be separated from contemporaneous inscriptions.

Material/history rule:

LATER_ASOKA_LEGEND
!=
ASHOKAN_INSCRIPTION.

## 9. Manuscript evidence

Gandhāran Buddhist manuscripts provide some of the earliest surviving physical Buddhist literary witnesses.

University of Washington Press's Gandhāran Buddhist Texts series describes birch-bark manuscripts in Gāndhārī and Kharoṣṭhī, including early Mahāyāna and Abhidharma materials.

Examples:
https://uwapress.uw.edu/book/9780295750736/three-early-mahayana-treatises-from-gandhara/
https://uwapress.uw.edu/book/9780295753843/a-gandhari-abhidharma-text/

Core distinction:

COMPOSITION_DATE
!=
MANUSCRIPT_DATE
!=
LATER_CANONICAL_STATUS.

## 10. Mahāyāna

Mahāyāna should not be treated as appearing all at once.

Research fronts:
- Prajñāpāramitā;
- bodhisattva path;
- multiple Buddha/buddha-field traditions;
- emptiness;
- devotional developments;
- early manuscript evidence.

Do not project mature Madhyamaka, Yogācāra, or Buddha-nature philosophy backward into every early Mahāyāna text.

## 11. Abhidharma

Abhidharma represents systematic analysis that develops differently across schools.

Guard:

ABHIDHARMA_SYSTEM
!=
EARLIEST_DISCOURSE_LAYER.

Its categories can preserve early analytical tendencies while also representing later scholastic elaboration.

## 12. Material and visual Buddhism

Stupas, reliquaries, inscriptions, donor records, and iconography provide evidence unavailable from doctrine alone.

Early visual representation can differ sharply by region and period.

Do not treat:
iconography
as:
a direct transcript of textual doctrine.

## 13. Comparative guards

Do not flatten:
- nirvāṇa into heaven;
- karma into divine punishment;
- rebirth into transmigration of a permanent soul;
- śūnyatā into nihilism;
- Buddha-nature into creator-God theology;
- bodhisattvas into saints without functional comparison;
- meditation into one uniform technique.
