# Christianity lane

This lane tracks how Yeshua/Jesus traditions develop from the earliest surviving Christian texts through canonical Gospel formation, competing early Christian literature, Christological development, and later institutional interpretation.

## Core rule

"Christianity says" is usually too coarse.

Ask instead:

- Which text?
- Approximately when was it composed?
- What earlier traditions may it use?
- Is the claim present in Paul, Mark, Matthew/Luke, John, or only later?
- Is it canonical, noncanonical, patristic, conciliar, medieval, or modern?

## Historical boundary

The earliest Jesus movement begins as a Jewish movement. This lane starts where claims about Yeshua and his significance are being transmitted by his followers; it does not retroactively make the historical Yeshua a member of a fully developed later Christianity.
