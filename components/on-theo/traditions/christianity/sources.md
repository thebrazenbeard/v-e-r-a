# Christianity lane — source spine

## Primary texts

### Earliest/canonical

- Paul's undisputed letters, especially 1 Corinthians, Galatians, Romans, Philippians, 1 Thessalonians.
- Gospel of Mark.
- Gospels of Matthew and Luke, especially infancy narratives in Matthew 1–2 and Luke 1–2.
- Gospel of John, especially John 1 and resurrection material involving Mary Magdalene in John 20.

### Selected noncanonical

- Gospel of Thomas.
- Gospel of Mary.
- Infancy Gospel / Protevangelium of James.
- Infancy Gospel of Thomas.
- Gospel of Peter.

## Cautions

Conventional dates are ranges and scholarly judgments, not timestamps embedded in the manuscripts. Synoptic-source theories remain debated. A later text can preserve early material, and an early text can still be interpretive.
