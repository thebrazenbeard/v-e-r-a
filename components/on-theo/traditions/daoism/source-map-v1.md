## Laozi / Daodejing

Stanford Encyclopedia of Philosophy:
https://plato.stanford.edu/entries/laozi/

Key source-critical controls:
- received text is not assumed to be a single-author sixth-century-BCE document;
- Guodian and Mawangdui manuscripts demonstrate early textual variation;
- received 81-chapter form is a later stabilized textual state.

Rule:
RECEIVED_LAOZI != SINGLE_ORIGINAL_ARCHETYPE.

## Buddhist contact

Track:
- translation terminology;
- monastic institutions;
- meditation vocabulary;
- cosmological adaptation;
- polemic and mutual borrowing.

Do not infer pre-Buddhist identity from later overlap.

## High-risk translations

- dao = "God";
- wu = "nothingness" without context;
- qi = "energy" in modern physics sense;
- wuwei = literal total inactivity;
- ziran = modern "nature" without semantic qualification.

Each requires source-context control.
