# Hindu Traditions — Master Research Lane V1

Status: FOUNDATION / MULTI-TRADITION CONTROL

## Scope

"Hinduism" is a broad retrospective category covering many textual, ritual, philosophical, devotional, and regional traditions.

Core guard:

VEDIC_RELIGION != ALL_LATER_HINDUISM.

No single text, deity, philosophical school, or period should be made representative of the whole tradition.

## Vedic source stack

The Vedic Heritage Portal maintained under the Indira Gandhi National Centre for the Arts provides institutional access to:
- Rigveda;
- Yajurveda traditions;
- Samaveda traditions;
- Atharvaveda traditions;
- Brahmanas;
- Aranyakas;
- Upanishads;
- ritual and recitation traditions.

Sources:
https://vedicheritage.gov.in/

Research rule:
Samhita, Brahmana, Aranyaka, Upanishadic, epic, Purāṇic, Tantric, and later devotional strata must remain chronologically distinct.

## Core diachronic lanes

### Vedic religion
Priority:
- Agni;
- Indra;
- Soma;
- Varuṇa;
- Uṣas;
- Aśvins;
- sacrifice / yajña;
- ṛta;
- priestly roles;
- ancestor rites;
- death and afterlife language.

### Upanishadic developments
Track emergence and variation in:
- ātman;
- brahman;
- karma;
- rebirth;
- liberation.

Do not assume all Upanishads teach one identical metaphysics.

### Epic traditions
Mahābhārata and Rāmāyaṇa require:
- compositional layering;
- manuscript/recension control;
- later theological accretion tracking.

### Bhagavad Gītā
Treat as embedded within Mahābhārata and distinguish its theological synthesis from earlier Vedic materials.

### Purāṇic and sectarian traditions
Track separately:
- Vaiṣṇava;
- Śaiva;
- Śākta;
- Smārta;
- regional devotional traditions.

### Tantra
Do not use "Tantra" as a generic label for all esoteric or sexual religious practice.
Bind claims to specific texts, lineages, dates, and ritual systems.

## Philosophical systems

Separate proposition maps for:
- Sāṃkhya;
- Yoga;
- Nyāya;
- Vaiśeṣika;
- Mīmāṃsā;
- Vedānta families.

Within Vedānta distinguish:
- Advaita;
- Viśiṣṭādvaita;
- Dvaita;
- other schools.

Guard:
BRAHMAN != AUTOMATICALLY PERSONAL_CREATOR_GOD.

## Comparative controls

Do not flatten:
- Vedic deities into later Purāṇic forms;
- ātman into a Western "soul" without semantic qualification;
- mokṣa into heaven;
- avatāra into incarnation without proposition-level comparison;
- cyclical cosmology into simulation theory;
- māyā into "the universe is fake" without school-specific control.

## Material-evidence lanes

Priorities:
- Indus/Harappan religion claims must remain highly controlled;
- early Vedic archaeology;
- temple development;
- inscriptions;
- iconography;
- cult images;
- pilgrimage sites;
- regional material variation.

Guard:
HARAPPAN ICONOGRAPHY != LATER HINDU DEITY IDENTIFICATION unless independent evidence closes the gap.
