## Source-critical priorities

### Rigveda
Use for:
early Vedic deity, ritual, praise, cosmic-order, sacrificial vocabulary.

Do not project later:
- Trimurti models;
- mature avatar theology;
- Advaita;
- sectarian temple systems

back into Rigvedic hymns.

### Upanishads
Track text by text:
- atman;
- brahman;
- karma;
- rebirth;
- liberation.

Do not assume one Upanishadic metaphysics.

### Epics
Mahabharata and Ramayana require compositional-layer control.

### Puranas
Use as later mythic/theological corpora, not direct Vedic-age witnesses.

## Material evidence

- Vedic archaeology;
- inscriptional evidence;
- temple history;
- iconographic development;

HARAPPAN_FIGURE != SHIVA
without source-specific continuity evidence.

## Comparative risks

- atman = Western soul;
- brahman = personal creator God;
- maya = simulation;
- moksha = heaven;
- avatar = Christian incarnation;
- yajna = generic sacrifice.

All require proposition-level semantic comparison.
