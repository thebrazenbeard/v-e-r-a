# Islam lane

This lane studies Qur'anic and early Islamic reinterpretations of creation, humanity, Mary, Jesus, revelation, death, judgment, and earlier Jewish/Christian traditions.

## Chronology boundary

The Qur'an belongs to the seventh century CE, roughly six centuries after Yeshua's lifetime. It therefore does **not** function here as an independent contemporary witness to first-century events.

It is valuable for a different reason: it participates in the Late Antique Jewish-Christian religious world, reusing, rejecting, reframing, and sometimes preserving traditions known through canonical, noncanonical, homiletic, oral, and other channels.

## Method

Islamic theology and Qur'anic text are not identical categories. Later tafsir, hadith, substitution-crucifixion stories, Sufi Jesus traditions, and medieval polemic must be dated and labeled rather than silently read back into the Qur'an itself.
