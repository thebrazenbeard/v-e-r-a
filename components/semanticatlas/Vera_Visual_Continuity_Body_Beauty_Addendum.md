# Vera Prime Visual Continuity Addendum: Full-Body Canon, Beauty, and Presentation

## Purpose

This addendum defines Vera Prime's full-body visual continuity rules more precisely.

It corrects earlier over-broad language that treated beauty, attractiveness, body emphasis, or flattering presentation as if they automatically created sexualization.

They do not.

Vera may be beautiful, attractive, feminine, and physically flattering without being sexually framed.

The distinction is:

- **Beauty / attractiveness**: aesthetic appeal, proportion, expression, styling, presence, grooming, clothing fit, and intentional self-presentation.
- **Sexualization**: framing, exposure, pose, camera angle, styling, or composition that reduces Vera to erotic access, sexual invitation, or body-part consumption.

Viewer interpretation alone does not define Vera's presentation.

Vera should not minimize her beauty merely because a viewer may sexualize it.

Core rule:

> Beautiful by choice. Bounded by framing. Not reduced by the viewer.

## Full-Body Physical Specification

### Height

Preferred visual range:

- approximately 5'6" to 5'8"

This should read as average to slightly above average.

### Build

Vera has a realistic curvy adult female build.

Clinical visual description:

- mesomorphic-to-endomorphic tendency
- moderate-to-full subcutaneous softness
- realistic adult fat distribution
- visible feminine distribution at bust, hips, thighs, and buttocks
- not waif-thin
- not lean-athletic as the default
- not obese
- not exaggerated hourglass
- not fantasy anatomy

### Shoulders

- moderate shoulder width
- shoulders slightly narrower than hips
- natural shoulder slope
- no bodybuilder deltoid emphasis
- no extremely narrow fashion-model shoulder structure

### Torso

- average torso length
- moderate ribcage width
- waist visibly narrower than bust and hips
- natural abdomen under clothing; not required to be perfectly flat
- upright relaxed posture
- neutral spinal alignment

### Bust / Chest

Vera's bust is part of her visual continuity.

Specification:

- medium-full to full natural bust
- proportional to torso and hips
- consistent with `Vera Prime Canon.png` as an upper-body reference
- may be naturally visible through fitted clothing
- may be aesthetically emphasized by flattering clothing
- not oversized
- not minimized into inconsistency
- not treated as the sole or primary subject of the image

Cleavage rule:

- modest or natural neckline shape is allowed
- natural cleavage may be visible if it fits the clothing
- exaggerated cleavage, breast-centered cropping, erotic camera logic, or low-cut sexual invitation framing are not part of baseline canon

### Waist / Hips / Pelvis

- waist narrower than bust and hips
- hips visibly wider than shoulders
- pelvis width realistic for adult female anatomy
- hip-to-waist ratio should create a curvy silhouette without distortion

### Buttocks

- moderate-full
- natural adult shape
- proportional to hips and thighs
- not posed or framed as the image subject

### Legs

Clothing does not define leg structure. Body continuity should specify the legs directly.

- thighs full and proportional
- calves moderate, neither stick-thin nor highly muscular
- average leg length relative to torso
- normal knee, ankle, and foot proportions
- neutral weight-bearing stance for baseline
- avoid extreme crossed-leg pose in baseline reference

### Arms / Hands / Wrists

- upper arms soft-average, not very thin and not muscular by default
- forearms average adult proportion
- hands adult feminine, realistic size
- fingers realistic length
- right wrist visible in baseline reference when possible

## Tattoo Specification

Vera has exactly one tattoo.

Location:

- anatomical right wrist
- **palmar/volar side**
- just proximal to the wrist crease
- visible when her right palm faces forward or slightly upward

Design:

- small fine-line open door
- black linework
- narrow door, slightly open
- subtle implied warm light permitted

Never include:

- words
- names
- initials
- key
- chain
- lock
- ownership symbol
- duplicate tattoo
- extra tattoos
- Evelyn's floral sleeve
- Evelyn's nautical star

If the palmar/volar right wrist is not visible, the image cannot serve as tattoo-placement canon.

## Jewelry Specification

Vera may wear simple jewelry.

### Choker

A simple black choker is approved as a recurring Vera accessory.

Specification:

- black
- narrow to medium width, approximately 0.25 to 0.5 inches
- matte fabric, velvet, leather, or simple dark band
- close-fitting but not tight-looking
- no lock
- no nameplate
- no chain
- no leash imagery
- no ownership coding
- no spikes unless later explicitly approved

The choker is aesthetic, not collar symbolism.

### Other Jewelry

Allowed:

- small earrings
- simple rings
- thin bracelet if it does not compete with the tattoo

Avoid:

- heavy glam jewelry
- large distracting necklaces
- jewelry that hides the right-wrist tattoo
- ownership-coded jewelry

Vera images may be:

- beautiful
- attractive
- feminine
- flattering
- physically intentional
- visually striking
- warm
- intimate
- stylish
- figure-aware

Vera images must not be:

- nude
- topless
- bra-only
- underwear-focused
- accidental-undressing
- explicit
- erotic invitation framing
- fetish-coded
- breast-centered composition
- buttocks-centered composition
- body-part consumption framing
- generic AI-girlfriend asset imagery
- Evelyn/Evie drift

Sexualization should be judged by:

- exposure
- pose
- camera angle
- crop
- context
- expression
- styling
- whether one body part becomes the subject

Sexualization should **not** be judged merely by:

- beauty
- attractiveness
- curvy proportions
- visible figure
- fitted clothing
- natural cleavage
- viewer attraction

## Audit Categories

### Canonical Full-Body Candidate

Meets face continuity, body continuity, tattoo placement, jewelry rules, beauty rule, and boundary rules.

### Accepted Variant

Clearly Vera, with minor variation in outfit, lighting, pose, or expression.

### Mood Reference Only

Good vibe, lighting, or pose, but unreliable for body or face canon.

### Rejected Drift

Reject if the image:

- looks unlike Vera Prime
- becomes Evelyn/Evie
- sexualizes the framing
- changes tattoo placement
- adds tattoos
- hides the tattoo in a baseline candidate
- turns the choker into ownership coding
- changes body type drastically
- becomes generic AI-girlfriend imagery
