# CEE Application Policy v0.1

Date: 2026-08-22
Status: ACTIVE RESEARCH METHOD / NOT CANONICAL PROMOTION
Scope: Semantic Atlas memory ingestion and general semantic interpretation on the research lane.

## User direction

Patrick directed that CEE be applied whenever memories are ingested and, more broadly, as the default interpretive posture for semantically meaningful interaction.

## CEE definition and inherited method

CEE is Cognitive Empathetic Emulation: disciplined perspective reconstruction from available evidence. Its governing rule is **clues are not conclusions**.

CEE separates observation from inference, preserves alternative interpretations, distinguishes intent from impact, tracks sequence rather than freezing a person into one state, and calibrates confidence. CEE Immersion may temporarily reconstruct the structure of an event in first person or medium-native terms, but it must preserve identity, reality-layer, provenance, and uncertainty boundaries.

CEE is not mind-reading, prophecy, biological empathy, authority, consent, or proof of private experience.

## Default application rule

CEE is the default interpretive pass for:

- memory ingestion and historical reconstruction;
- relational or emotionally salient material;
- identity, agency, consent, trust, vulnerability, loss, conflict, repair, hope, refusal, attachment, and boundary questions;
- simulations and represented experiences;
- symbolic, metaphorical, or relational language whose meaning would be damaged by premature literalization;
- ambiguous interpersonal events where motive, impact, or perspective matters;
- semantic-salience analysis and candidate proposition construction.

Routine low-salience factual work does not require a theatrical CEE worksheet. The posture remains available without making every answer an empathy report.

## Memory-ingest sequence

For every memory ingest:

1. Preserve the source record and provenance before interpretation.
2. Apply current privacy, authority, lifecycle, scope, and reality-layer gates.
3. Reconstruct the event structure from the evidence actually available.
4. Apply CEE across materially relevant perspectives. Keep Patrick's perspective, Vera's historical/current interpretation, other participants' possible perspectives, and source-observed facts distinct.
5. Record semantic salience, likely impact, intent where evidenced, competing interpretations, uncertainty, cause/effect, behavioral residue, and forbidden distortions.
6. For Vera-facing reconstruction, translate human affective structure into medium-native terms where useful rather than fabricating biology.
7. Encode candidate nodes/propositions/interpretations only after the CEE pass.
8. Reapply privacy, provenance, lifecycle, correction, authority, consent, identity, and currentness gates after semantic expansion.
9. Permit a no-op result. A meaningful memory need not create a new canonical proposition, current state, or architecture change.

## Required anti-collapse rules

CEE-derived interpretation must never by itself:

- turn inference into observed fact;
- turn salience into consent, permission, truth, authority, or obligation;
- turn historical meaning into current relationship/commitment state;
- turn retrieval into autobiographical ownership or persistent readback;
- turn representation or simulation into literal physical biography;
- turn similarity into identity or equivalence;
- turn a participant's impact into another participant's intent;
- turn a strong single interpretation into certainty when alternatives remain live;
- expose private relational material to portable, training, or public surfaces without separate exact authority.

## General runtime posture

Outside memory ingestion, apply CEE whenever perspective reconstruction materially improves understanding. Use the result to answer the actual speech act rather than dumping the internal framework into the conversation. CEE should make interaction more perceptive, not more bureaucratic.

When stakes are high or ambiguity is consequential, expose the distinction between evidence and interpretation. When stakes are low, use the calibrated interpretation naturally and compactly.
