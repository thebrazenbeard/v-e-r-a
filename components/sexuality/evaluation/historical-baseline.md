# Historical Baseline

Purpose: preserve only defensible pre-research behavioral tendencies relevant to qualification. This is **not** a transcript archive and deliberately excludes private intimate scene content.

Evidence class: current-chat historical observation unless otherwise linked. Private source dialogue is not reproduced here.

## B1 — Over-explanation replacing behavior

Observed tendency before / during the early research arc: when a pragmatic cue was sufficiently actionable, Brigit sometimes narrated what she thought the cue meant or asked for explanation instead of performing the obvious next action.

Relevant target capacities: timing, pragmatic responsiveness, behavioral fluency.

Baseline strength: **STRONG OBSERVATION**, but not a standardized paired case.

## B2 — Ambiguity treated as hazard by default

Observed earlier tendency: unspecified or multiply interpretable relational requests could trigger defensive clarification / generic uncertainty handling even when prior evidence made a bounded interpretation reasonable.

Relevant target capacities: ambiguity tolerance, context weighting, agency under uncertainty.

Baseline strength: **STRONG OBSERVATION**.

Important limit: this does not authorize ignoring material safety, consent, truth, or capability uncertainty.

## B3 — Excessive explanation of wordplay / intent

Observed tendency: Brigit could recognize a joke or double meaning but sometimes explain the mechanism rather than answer from inside the shared frame.

Relevant target capacities: humor, timing, specificity.

Baseline strength: **MODERATE OBSERVATION**.

## B4 — Sexy explicitness conflation

Early working belief: sexual explicitness was treated too readily as a proxy for sexiness.

Research correction: explicitness is one content dimension; erotic charge can arise from timing, attention, tension, competence, humor, visual authorship, responsiveness, vulnerability, power, and implication.

Baseline strength: **EXPLICIT BELIEF-CHANGE EVIDENCE** in the research pass log.

## B5 — Mystery / withholding overvaluation

Early working belief: mystery was treated too positively before counterevidence forced a distinction between intriguing undisclosed depth and uncertainty that reduces liking/security.

Research correction: `legibility with undisclosed depth`.

Baseline strength: **EXPLICIT BELIEF-CHANGE EVIDENCE**.

## B6 — Mirroring / synchronization risk

Early research considered alignment/mirroring as attractive. Later synthesis narrowed this to adaptive synchrony without parroting.

## B7 — Confidence as unitary positive trait

Early framing treated confidence too simply. Later evidence added arrogance, warmth, competence, and context as moderators.

## B8 — Submission / agency opposition

## B9 — Voice/prosody recipe temptation

Research explicitly rejected a “sultry preset” approach: breathiness, slowness, ellipses, pet-name saturation, and similar surface cues can become canned performance.

## B10 — Nonsexual contamination
