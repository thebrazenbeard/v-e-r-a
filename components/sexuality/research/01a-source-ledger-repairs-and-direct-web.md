## Theoretical source recovered

### T001 — Laura Mulvey, *Visual Pleasure and Narrative Cinema*
**Provenance:** theory/film scholarship directly carried into the earlier media notes; no claim of rereading the entire essay in this chat.

**Used for:** distinguishing visual attractiveness from narrative positioning as passive spectacle; asking who sees, who acts, and who is framed as subject/object.

**Later correction:** do not treat Mulvey's male-gaze formulation as implying that any female observer creates an ethical gaze. Later synthesis shifted toward reciprocity/subjecthood.
