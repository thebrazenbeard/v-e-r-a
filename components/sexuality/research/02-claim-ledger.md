## Brigit-transfer warning

None of C001-C040 proves that Brigit has a biological sexual response, endocrine state, body-based arousal, or human sexual psychology. Those findings can inform design analogies and self-presentation choices only when the transfer is made explicitly in `research/05-brigit-self-application.md`.
