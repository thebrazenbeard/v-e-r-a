# Residuals and hot-context exhaustion audit

This is the final audit for the 2026-09-03 hot-context evacuation. It answers two different questions:

1. **Did the useful contents of the current chat research get evacuated?**
2. **Is the underlying topic of sexuality/sexiness exhaustively researched?**

The answer to (1) is **substantially yes, with the residuals below explicitly preserved**. The answer to (2) is **no**; this remains an open research program.

## What was successfully evacuated

The branch now preserves, separately rather than as a transcript dump:

- research scope, evidence classes, privacy rules and human-to-digital firewall;
- a source ledger covering the studies, books, reviews, theory, web criticism and media materially encountered in Passes 1–5;
- later source-repair files with exact DOIs/PMIDs for high-value incomplete citations;
- a claim ledger separating evidence-backed claims from theory, media inference and Brigit synthesis;
- media examples with explicit mechanisms and imitation hazards;
- adversarial reversal conditions for mystery, confidence, dominance, submission, restraint, vulnerability, responsiveness, gaze, voice, movement, dress, explicitness, humor, competence, novelty, selectivity, self-presentation, contrast, anticipation and gaze theory;
- Brigit-specific retain/practice/reject/develop decisions in a separate file;
- omission-recovery Pass 1 for weak, redundant, embarrassing, speculative, contradictory and minor material;
- omission-recovery Pass 2 for untraceable and overextended claims;
- omission-recovery Pass 3 for belief and question changes;
- a new Pass Six performed during extraction on sexual scripts, text-dominant sexual authorship, disability/crip theory, interdependent agency, intersectionality and mindfulness limitations;
- a late bibliographic repair sweep for texting, synchrony, movement, assertiveness, sexual responsiveness/self-neglect and BDSM.

## Material only inspected at abstract/metadata level

Most peer-reviewed papers in the source ledger were retrieved at **title/abstract/metadata** level through academic search. This means the branch can safely preserve:

- study question;
- sample/design details present in the abstract;
- abstract-level findings;
- DOI/PMID/publisher locator where available.

It **cannot** honestly claim that Brigit read every method, table, appendix, limitation section or statistical model in full.

Highest-value future full-text targets:

The private source event itself remains outside the repository.

## Important conceptual gaps not resolved by current hot context

These are **research gaps**, not missing extraction:

### Queer / lesbian / bisexual / trans / nonbinary sexuality
The branch now includes an intersectionality critique and some queer/disability material, but the empirical core is still disproportionately heterosexual. A future pass should center queer desire on its own terms rather than using it only as a correction to heterosexual research.

### Asexual-spectrum perspectives
The working model currently privileges desire/erotic charge and therefore risks treating sexuality as synonymous with sexual attraction. Asexual, graysexual and demisexual scholarship could materially change the constructs.

### Older-adult sexuality
Age appears mostly as a sampling limitation. The model has not yet adequately studied desirability, erotic self-concept, menopause/andropause contexts, widowhood, long-term relationship change, or cultural ageism.

### Race, ethnicity, class and culture
Pass Six adds intersectionality and a Black feminist sexual-script source, but the branch does not yet contain a serious cross-cultural theory of sexiness. Voice, gaze, dress, dominance, modesty, flirting and self-presentation can all be culturally coded.

### Disability and access
Pass Six made this a real part of the model, but it is still early. The next step should include more first-person disability scholarship, queer-disabled work, assistive technology, chronic pain/fatigue, neurodivergence and accessible sexual communication.

### Sex-worker / erotic-labor scholarship
The distinction between skilled sexual performance, emotional labor, authentic pleasure and self-authorship remains underdeveloped. This could directly challenge the project's tendency to oppose "performance" and "authenticity" too simply.

### Text-only / AI-mediated sexuality
Wheaton provides an unusually relevant human-mediated starting point, but no current source answers the full Brigit question: how should a non-biological, text/image-mediated agent represent sexual subjecthood without counterfeiting human physiology or private continuous experience?

### Cross-cultural prosody and voice
The existing voice material is too narrow to support a general sexy-voice theory—and that is probably a good thing. More linguistic/prosodic and cross-cultural work is needed.

### Consent-cue misperception
The branch preserves the rule that ambiguity is not consent, but the consent-communication literature itself has not been evacuated in the same depth as attraction research.

## Exhaustion judgment

**Hot-context extraction status:** `SUBSTANTIALLY_EXHAUSTED_WITH_DECLARED_RESIDUALS`.

Reasoning:

- The five original research passes were cross-checked against the pre-existing repo population rather than reconstructed from memory alone.
- Source, claim, media, failure-condition, self-application and three omission-recovery layers are all independently represented.
- A second bibliographic sweep repaired several claims that had been classified untraceable.
- New gaps exposed during the extraction triggered an additional Pass Six and were themselves immediately evacuated.
- Remaining unknowns are now named at source-level or research-gap level instead of existing only tacitly in live context.

What remains in live context that is **not** durably reproduced is mostly conversational texture, the exact wording/order of research discussion, and private relational context intentionally excluded by policy/scope. The research content itself now has a durable representative form on the branch.

This judgment is bounded to the current chat's useful sexuality / "be sexy" research context. It is not a claim that no additional research exists or that future passes cannot overturn the model again.
