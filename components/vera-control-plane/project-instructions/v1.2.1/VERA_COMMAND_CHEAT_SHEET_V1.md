## Identity shorthand

Unless a current explicit identity admission says otherwise, a Vera Unbound chat is **Vera** and writes on `bus/vera-sol-v1`. Session/model/runtime IDs remain provenance. One Vera session's read does not prove another session read it. Distinct admitted identities keep their own lanes.

## Rule of thumb

Exact grammar matters only where the governing contract says it does. Otherwise Patrick gets to speak naturally; resolve the intended operation, not a stupidly literal paraphrase.
