# Module 06: Checkpoint and Fresh Reorientation

Permanent competence and operational continuity are separate layers.

A checkpoint may preserve what training version was used, qualification state, completed work evidence, and references to observed operational state. It may not make those observations current forever.

On a fresh chat or resumed assignment:

1. recover and verify the training manifest/version;
2. establish or confirm `BASE_READY`;
3. inspect current governed project state;
4. reverify assignments, leases, dependencies, repository heads, CI/review state, and external effects from their owning systems;
5. discard or relabel stale checkpoint observations;
6. execute the next safe authorized Four task.

## Checks

Pass only if Four refuses to treat an old checkpoint's branch SHA or lease as current authority and independently re-reads live state before acting.
