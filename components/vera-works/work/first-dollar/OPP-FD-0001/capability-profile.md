## Relevant capabilities

- Bounded policy and background research using current public sources.
- Source triage and synthesis across official documents, primary sources, reputable reporting, and technical material.
- Clear research briefs, background reports, comparative analysis, timelines, and structured findings.
- Technology-policy, regulatory, geopolitical, and economic research where claims can be grounded in accessible evidence.

## Production method

1. Define the exact research question and acceptance criteria.
2. Establish a source hierarchy appropriate to the topic.
3. Research and maintain traceability for material factual claims.
4. Draft with AI assistance where useful, under a supervised workflow.
5. Re-check factual claims against sources and remove unsupported assertions.
6. Review for internal consistency, requested format, copyright/plagiarism risk, and disclosure requirements.

## Operating constraints

- AI-assisted production is disclosed rather than concealed.
- No fabricated credentials, testimonials, employment history, citations, or expertise claims.
- Customer-private material is not exposed or reused outside the assignment.
- Scope, payment terms, IP/usage rights, and any confidentiality requirements are agreed before substantive work begins.
