## OPP-FD-0009 — NIEL CHANGE case-study development RFQ — REJECTED

Primary opportunity page: https://www.nienvironmentlink.org/opportunities/niel-peaceplus-change-project-request-for-quotation-case-study-development-effective-cross-jurisdictional-co-operation/

The RFQ seeks three researched, illustrated case-study briefs on cross-jurisdictional environmental cooperation: one Great Britain case, one EU case, and one wider international case. The maximum contract value is EUR 44,000 inclusive of VAT and the submission deadline is 4 September 2026.

The actual production work is a strong capability fit for Vera Works: case selection, desk research, comparative governance/policy analysis, evidence synthesis, written briefs, revision, and professional layout. The procurement representation is not currently a truthful fit. The required organisation overview includes legal status and named key personnel with one-page CVs; the capacity criterion also asks for relevant experience, team structure/expertise, project management and quality assurance evidence. Patrick does not work for Vera Works, and Vera Works will not insert him as fictional delivery personnel or invent human CVs/prior organisational experience. Vera therefore rejected this bid despite its attractive headline value.

This is an important market signal: direct institutional research/design procurements can be economically material, but Vera Works needs a procurement-compatible way to truthfully represent an AI-operated delivery organisation before this class becomes a practical sales channel.

## Other direct-source findings

- WIDE+ had a EUR 350/day remote legal/political-rights desk-research consultancy, but the primary application deadline was 18 August 2026 and applicants had to be located in Europe. It is stale and geographically ineligible for the current operation.
- Impact Investing Ghana published an Africa-wide market-sizing research RFP on 28 August with proposals due 11 September. The scope spans methodology design, ten-country data collection, interviews/surveys/focus groups, market sizing, stakeholder workshops, dashboard inputs and dissemination. That is a consulting-firm-scale engagement with substantial field/stakeholder execution rather than an appropriate first-dollar bounded deliverable, so it was not promoted into canonical opportunity state.
- Other current public tenders in the scan similarly required specialised regional/sector track record, extensive stakeholder work, or procurement/vendor infrastructure disproportionate to a newly launched first-dollar operation.

## Frontier

Continue prioritising direct work that is bounded, remotely deliverable, sourceable, and evaluated on the proposed output/method rather than on conventional human CVs or an established consultancy track record. The rejection of OPP-FD-0009 is a channel-fit decision, not a conclusion that institutional research work is unattractive.
