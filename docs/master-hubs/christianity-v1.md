## Core chronology rule

The historical Yeshua/Jesus belongs first inside first-century Jewish history.

Later Christianity must not be projected backward as if:
- Nicene formulations;
- later Trinitarian terminology;
- Marian dogma;
- medieval sacramental systems;
- denominational theology

were already explicit in the earliest sources.

Core rule:

HISTORICAL_YESHUA
!=
FULLY_DEVELOPED_LATER_CHRISTIANITY.

## Primary source sequence

At minimum distinguish:
- undisputed Pauline letters;
- Mark;
- Matthew;
- Luke-Acts;
- John;
- other New Testament writings;
- Apostolic Fathers;
- noncanonical gospels and acts;
- patristic polemic;
- conciliar formulations;
- medieval developments.

Do not collapse composition date, manuscript date, and later canonical status.

## Comparison guards

Do not infer lineage from motif similarity alone for:
- dying/rising deities;
- divine sons;
- virgin births;
- sacred meals;
- resurrection;
- cosmic judgment;
- Logos;
- savior figures.

Every transmission claim requires:
chronology + contact + carrier + semantic fit + source-specific evidence.
