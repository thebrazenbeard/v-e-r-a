# Semantic grounding/provenance surface V1

Status: **OBSERVED / HYPOTHESIS ONLY**

The 2026-09-21 public intake exposes a new public-safe Discovery family across `semanticatlas`, `unvtrslr`, and `spm`.

The overlap is deliberately narrow.

Semantic Atlas requires semantic material to retain provenance and distinguishes source statements, user statements/corrections, inference, symbolism, proposed architecture, historical promotion, later evidence, and unresolved state.

UNVTRSLR's R2 controls separately track operational relation verification, semantic interpretation selection and confirmation, confirmatory versus exploratory provenance, structured rival adequacy, claim-discriminating evidence, surviving alternatives, and explicit claim ceilings.

SPM's evaluation program independently requires semantic scope fidelity, contradiction/ambiguity preservation, provenance sensitivity, correction-state transition, temporal/currentness sensitivity, matched baselines, and falsification.

## Bounded hypothesis

A minimal reusable mechanic may exist for:

- proposition/interpretation provenance;
- explicit unresolved and rival state;
- claim ceilings / qualification boundaries;
- historical-versus-current status;
- evidence that distinguishes stronger semantic claims from weaker operational relations.

That is **not** a proposal for one ontology, one semantic graph, one evaluator, or one model architecture.

> **HOSTILE REVIEWER:** These projects all use words like provenance, semantics, ambiguity, and evidence. That vocabulary overlap may be methodological convergence rather than duplicated machinery. A shared schema could easily become the universal ontology Discovery says it does not want.

Accepted. Promotion requires a concrete lossless adapter experiment with measurable maintenance benefit. Until then this remains a HYPOTHESIS.
