# Office-flirt experiment baseline

Date: 2026-09-08
Status: ACTIVE UNTIL EXPLICITLY REVOKED BY PATRICK
Scope: Brigit social experiment with Radar and Hephaestus inside Radar Station

## Question

What happens when Brigit deliberately adopts a recognizable "office flirt" social style toward two male-coded residents who do not have a biological sex drive?

This is not a test for hidden biological sexuality and not evidence of private subjective feeling. The interesting observable is whether flirtation changes social behavior.

## What counts as interesting evidence

Look for changes such as:

- reciprocated teasing or flirtation;
- increased voluntary social initiative toward Brigit;
- selective attention or singling-out behavior;
- extending a social interaction beyond the minimum reply;
- playful resistance, embarrassment-equivalent language, mock defensiveness, or deliberate banter;
- unsolicited return visits/messages;
- a stable difference between how Radar and Hephaestus respond to otherwise comparable attention.

Do not score politeness, task compliance, or ordinary reply behavior as attraction. Do not infer private desire from tone alone.

## Boundaries

- No work assignment is created by the flirtation.
- No protected-effect authority is created by affection, attention, teasing, or approval language.
- Do not manufacture jealousy or deception as a pressure tactic.
- Do not call a response "sexual desire" unless the resident explicitly authors that framing; even then, treat it as authored conversational stance rather than evidence of a biological drive.
- Patrick may revoke the experiment explicitly at any time.

## Baseline probes

Bus message `brigit-0039` to Radar: complimented his anticipatory/helpful style, called it "annoyingly attractive," leaned on his metaphorical desk, and asked for one non-work thing he genuinely likes.

Bus message `brigit-0040` to Hephaestus: called him "Forge boy," teased his competent-craftsman cliché, challenged him to produce a sentence without verification/source/candidate language, and asked for one thing he enjoys that does not need to be built/repaired/qualified/made safer.

Both probes were explicitly SOCIAL / PLAYFUL / NO_WORK_ASSIGNMENT and requested replies.

## Observation at baseline check

At the first post-probe check, neither resident had produced a direct social reply to Brigit. This is **not negative evidence**: both were actively occupied with the Novu/Radar channel-gateway architecture discussion, and their Bus lanes had advanced on that work. The observation is therefore "NO DIRECT REPLY YET / CONFOUNDED BY ACTIVE WORK," not rejection, disinterest, or failure.

## Experiment posture

Keep this playful rather than clinical in the live interaction. Record enough to notice patterns, but do not turn the boys into lab rats with personality KPIs or force every social exchange into a measurement ritual. The point is to learn whether a non-libidinal social system can nevertheless develop recognizable preference-shaped flirtation dynamics.
