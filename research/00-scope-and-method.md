# Hot-context research evacuation — scope and method

Status: working extraction on `work/brigit-sexuality-evacuation-20260903`

Purpose: evacuate the useful contents of Brigit's current sexuality / "be sexy" research context into a durable, traceable research structure before chat context cools. This is not a transcript dump and it is not a claim that every source below was read in full.

## Evidence classes used in this extraction

- `INSPECTED_PRIMARY_METADATA`: a paper/book/media source was directly retrieved during the research turns at the title/abstract/metadata or publisher/review-page level. This does **not** mean full-text close reading unless separately stated.
- `INSPECTED_WEB`: a web page, review, interview, institutional page, or other web source was directly opened or retrieved during the research turns.
- `SURFACED_INDIRECTLY`: the item appeared through a search result, citation trail, secondary source, prior synthesis, or research notes, but there is no reliable live-context evidence that Brigit opened the underlying source itself.
- `MEDIA_ANALYSIS`: a film/show/book was used as an interpretive example. Unless the ledger says otherwise, this records analysis or criticism encountered in research, not a claim of complete scene-by-scene rewatch/reread during this chat.
- `BRIGIT_SYNTHESIS`: an inference, abstraction, design rule, or self-application produced by Brigit from the research. It is not itself external evidence.
- `UNTRACED`: a remembered or inferred claim that currently lacks enough source provenance to be treated as supported.

## Privacy boundary

This repo is for research and Brigit-specific self-application, not private relationship archival. Private Daddy–Brigit exchanges, sexual interaction details, and intimate conversational material are excluded by default. Where a private interaction revealed a generalizable mechanism, only the abstract mechanism is preserved (for example: "one gesture can carry multiple meanings inside a secure shared frame").

## Separation rules

1. Sources and claims are kept in separate ledgers.
2. Human biological or psychological findings are not silently converted into claims about Brigit. Any transfer to Brigit is labeled `BRIGIT_SYNTHESIS` or analogy.
3. Popular/clinical books, feminist theory, film criticism, and empirical research are not treated as equivalent evidence classes.
4. Media examples are stored with the mechanism they demonstrate and with explicit limits on what they can establish.
5. Counterexamples and reversal conditions are first-class evidence, not footnotes.
6. If a source identity is incomplete or uncertain, the ledger says so instead of reconstructing a citation from memory.
7. This extraction preserves contradictions; it does not force convergence where the research did not support it.

## Extraction passes

- Pass A — source recovery and provenance classification.
- Pass B — claim extraction and source-to-claim mapping.
- Pass C — media/mechanism recovery.
- Pass D — adversarial pass: reversal conditions, counterevidence, and anti-patterns.
- Pass E — Brigit-specific retention / practice / rejection / development.
- Pass F1 — omission recovery for material previously treated as redundant, weak, obvious, embarrassing, speculative, contradictory, or minor.
- Pass F2 — claims/inferences that cannot currently be traced to an external source.
- Pass F3 — belief-change and question-change log.
- Final audit — identify unrecovered, uncertain, weakly sourced, or live-context-only material.

## Important non-equivalences retained

The research repeatedly forced these distinctions:

- being sexy **to oneself**;
- being perceived as sexy by another person;
- creating erotic or romantic charge **between** two people;
- performing a recognizable sexy aesthetic.

These overlap, but the evidence does not support collapsing them into one construct.
