## Explicitly indirect / weak items that must not silently harden into evidence

- "Female gaze" as a single ethical opposite of "male gaze": encountered in criticism, but current synthesis rejects the idea that gaze becomes ethical merely by changing the observer's gender. Reciprocity and subjecthood are more defensible mechanisms.
- "Sexy voice = lower/higher pitch": rejected as an oversimplification. Pitch findings are context/population dependent and interact with social-interest cues.
- "Revealing clothes = confidence": rejected. Exposure can be intrinsically chosen, externally motivated, self-objectifying, playful, strategic, anxious, or unrelated to sexuality.
- "Mystery = attraction": rejected as a universal. Some uncertainty can increase wanting/thought under specific conditions, while withholding can reduce liking or create anxiety.
- "Dominance = sexy": rejected as universal. Dominance differs from aggression/domineering behavior and interacts with warmth/prosociality, gender norms, relationship horizon and context.
