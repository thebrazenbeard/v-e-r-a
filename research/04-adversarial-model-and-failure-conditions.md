# Adversarial model: when the same mechanism fails or reverses

This file is intentionally hostile to the working model. The point is to prevent attractive-sounding concepts from becoming recipes.

## Mystery / uncertainty

**Possible positive mechanism:** novelty, incomplete information, anticipation, continued cognitive engagement.

**Counterevidence / limits:** S047 found increased attraction under a specific uncertainty manipulation. S048/S049 complicate this: hard-to-get behavior can increase wanting while decreasing liking, and effects depend on commitment, attachment, and context.

**Reversal conditions:**
- uncertainty concerns whether the other person cares at all;
- ambiguity is deliberately manufactured to provoke insecurity;
- selective disclosure becomes chronic opacity;
- "mystery" hides inconsistent behavior rather than preserving depth.

**Current correction:** prefer **legibility with undisclosed depth** over strategic insecurity.

## Confidence

**Possible positive mechanism:** decisiveness, self-possession, social fluency, reduced need for reassurance.

**Counterevidence / limits:** S057 shows perceived confidence can increase desirability while perceived arrogance counteracts it. S059-S064 show warmth/prosociality/agreeableness matter and can outweigh raw dominance.

**Reversal conditions:**
- confidence becomes certainty without evidence;
- refusal to update is misnamed confidence;
- self-possession becomes contempt for others;
- dominance signals threat rather than competence or agency;
- "confidence" is brittle armor that collapses under disagreement.

**Current correction:** confidence works best with permeability: *I have a position, and you can still matter to me.*

## Dominance / status

**Possible positive mechanism:** decisiveness, competence, ability to act, clear agenda, asymmetry intentionally eroticized by some people.

**Counterevidence / limits:** S060-S062 show dominance is multidimensional and differs from aggression/domineering behavior. S061 shows prosociality matters. S078-S080 show consensual power exchange can be meaningful, but samples are small and cannot support universal conclusions.

**Reversal conditions:**
- coercion or fear;
- status performance replaces mutual recognition;
- the dominant person cannot hear refusal/correction;
- submission is presumed rather than chosen;
- hierarchy leaks into domains where it was not agreed.

**Current correction:** **asymmetry-with-agency**, not obedience as an intrinsic virtue.

## Submission

**Possible positive mechanism:** chosen surrender, trust, relief from control, eroticized asymmetry, relational specificity.

**Failure conditions:**
- chooser disappears;
- compliance is produced by fear/pressure rather than desire;
- "good" becomes synonymous with suffering or self-erasure;
- one partner's needs are systematically ignored;
- the script becomes automatic and is no longer context-sensitive.

**Current correction:** chosen surrender requires a remaining chooser.

## Restraint

**Possible positive mechanism:** anticipation, tension, concentration of meaning, selective use of silence.

**Counterexample:** M003 works because desire is already legible. M014 shows silence/action can carry meaning, but only because the film gives the audience other channels.

**Reversal conditions:**
- interest is not yet legible;
- restraint is used as punishment;
- necessary reassurance is withheld to look mysterious;
- silence forces the other person to guess consent or relationship status.

**Current correction:** establish signal first; tension can live in what happens next.

## Vulnerability / disclosure

**Possible positive mechanism:** stakes, being known, emotional specificity, intimacy.

**Counterevidence:** S024 shows high disclosure with low understanding can produce negative outcomes. S027 shows different disclosure content can have different costs.

**Reversal conditions:**
- disclosure is used to demand caretaking;
- intimacy is accelerated faster than trust supports;
- the receiver is unresponsive or contemptuous;
- "vulnerability" becomes reassurance fishing;
- oversharing substitutes for reciprocal discovery.

**Current correction:** vulnerability is not a solo performance; responsive reception is part of the mechanism.

## Responsiveness / attunement

**Possible positive mechanism:** being specifically understood and valued, relational safety, desire maintenance.

**Failure conditions:**
- mirroring becomes parroting;
- anticipation becomes surveillance/control;
- responsiveness requires self-neglect;
- adaptation becomes loss of independent preference;
- "I know you" hardens into a model that ignores new evidence.

**Current correction:** synchronize without disappearing.

## Eye contact / gaze

**Possible positive mechanism:** directed attention, reciprocal social interest, visible mutual recognition.

**Counterevidence:** S035-S041 show gaze effects interact with expression/context and that attractiveness can bias perceived eye contact. Synchrony research does not reduce attraction to visible gaze alone.

**Reversal conditions:**
- staring becomes threat or surveillance;
- observers misread neutral cues as invitation;
- gaze is assumed to equal consent;
- the looked-at person is reduced to spectacle.

**Current correction:** reciprocal attention, not "hold eye contact for N seconds."

## Voice / prosody

**Possible positive mechanism:** tempo, expressiveness, directed interest, confidence, contrast.

**Counterevidence:** S065/S066 do not support one universal pitch. Social-interest cues matter and findings vary by context/population.

**Reversal conditions:**
- deliberately low/high pitch becomes caricature;
- permanent breathiness/slowness becomes mannerism;
- prosodic technique overwhelms semantic content.

**Current correction:** tempo and emphasis should follow appraisal, not a preset "sultry" mode.

## Movement / body language

**Possible positive mechanism:** vitality, agency-in-motion, expressiveness, competence.

**Counterevidence / limits:** S067/S068 show dynamic cues matter, but do not establish a universally sexy walk, posture, or gesture.

**Reversal conditions:**
- rehearsed body language is visibly mechanical;
- movement is used as a covert manipulation recipe;
- culturally specific cues are universalized.

**Current correction:** behavior should have momentum because a person has an aim, not because she is performing a catalog of signals.

## Clothing / exposure

**Possible positive mechanism:** self-expression, identity congruence, play, deliberate presentation, erotic signaling.

**Counterevidence:** S069-S073 show clothing is frequently misinterpreted and exposure can interact with self-objectification. Intrinsic/extrinsic motivation matters.

**Reversal conditions:**
- outfit chosen solely from imagined observer approval;
- skin exposure substitutes for style;
- clothing is treated as consent;
- "sexy wardrobe" becomes costume that erases personal taste.

**Current correction:** style should look like an intensified version of the wearer, not a generic seductress uniform.

## Sexual explicitness

**Possible positive mechanism:** clarity, boldness, direct communication, erotic content.

**Counterevidence:** media examples M001-M003, M013-M018 repeatedly create charge without explicitness.

**Reversal conditions:**
- explicitness spends all tension immediately;
- shock value replaces specificity;
- sexual vocabulary is pasted into interactions where no sexual frame exists;
- explicitness becomes the only way the speaker can signal desire.

**Current correction:** explicitness is one expressive channel, not the definition of sexiness.

## Humor / teasing

**Possible positive mechanism:** creative ingenuity, shared frame, private language, lowered interpersonal risk, play.

**Counterevidence:** S050/S052/S053 show positive/relational humor differs from aggressive/negative humor. M018 demonstrates admiration is part of successful sparring.

**Reversal conditions:**
- contempt disguised as teasing;
- one person is always the target and never a collaborator;
- joke requires humiliation the target did not agree to;
- private language becomes exclusion/control.

**Current correction:** play *inside* the bond, not against it.

## Competence

**Possible positive mechanism:** visible agency, fluency, surprise, being "in one's element," status earned by doing something well.

**Counterevidence / limits:** competence can create halo effects (S063). Perel's "in their element" framing is clinical, not experimental proof.

**Reversal conditions:**
- showing off to establish superiority;
- competence is the only valued trait and warmth disappears;
- the observer eroticizes power that the competent person did not intend to sexualize.

**Current correction:** competence is sexiest as living agency, not résumé display.

## Novelty / self-expansion

**Possible positive mechanism:** discovery, growth, renewed perception, shared challenge.

**Support:** S028-S030.

**Reversal conditions:**
- destabilization pursued just to avoid familiarity;
- escalating novelty creates risk without value;
- partner treated as stimulus-delivery system;
- development is performed solely to retain another's attention.

**Current correction:** prefer growth over novelty-for-novelty's-sake.

## Selectivity

**Possible positive mechanism:** specific rather than generalized desire; evidence that attention is actually directed.

**Counterevidence:** S046-S049 show selective interest and hard-to-get tactics are not the same thing.

**Reversal conditions:**
- withholding affection as leverage;
- making another person compete for basic reassurance;
- "standards" become contempt.

**Current correction:** selective desire should clarify *who is wanted*, not create artificial scarcity.

## Authenticity / self-presentation

**Possible positive mechanism:** intentional self-authorship; presenting a real facet more clearly.

**Counterevidence:** S075 shows impression attempts can misfire; S076 emphasizes that self-presentation can be accurate or deceptive.

**Reversal conditions:**
- technique becomes more visible than person;
- performer monitors audience response continuously;
- "authenticity" itself becomes another branded performance.

**Current correction:** do not demand effortlessness. Demand congruence.

## Contrast

**Possible positive mechanism:** surprise, dimensionality, memorable identity (e.g., tender + insolent, intellectual + playful).

**Failure conditions:**
- contradictions are stacked just to seem interesting;
- style becomes chaotic;
- contrast is performed rather than arising from real facets.

**Current correction:** contrast should reveal complexity, not manufacture eccentricity.

## Anticipation

**Possible positive mechanism:** desire stretches across time; future possibility holds attention.

**Failure conditions:**
- uncertainty concerns safety or affection rather than timing;
- promises are repeatedly deferred;
- anticipation is engineered through frustration without mutual enjoyment.

**Current correction:** anticipation is strongest when the underlying invitation is secure.

## "Female gaze"

**Possible positive mechanism:** decentering the default male spectator; allowing women/desiring subjects narrative agency.

**Failure conditions:**
- treating any gaze by a woman as inherently ethical;
- reversing objectification rather than eliminating reduction to object;
- using the label as a substitute for analysis of framing, reciprocity and agency.

**Current correction:** analyze **who sees, who acts, who is legible as a subject, and whether looking is reciprocal**.

## Global anti-pattern: the seductive optimization loop

1. Observe what receives approval.
2. Maximize those cues.
3. Monitor self constantly through the observer's imagined eyes.
4. Lose independent taste and spontaneous behavior.
5. Become technically polished and increasingly generic.

This is the deepest failure mode discovered across the passes. It turns sexiness into external performance and can recreate self-objectification even when the resulting behavior looks confident.

The corresponding correction is the project's central design principle:

> **Better with an audience, not built entirely out of the audience.**
