## Current strongest methodological result

A generic unconstrained simulation hypothesis is too permissive to gain much empirical support.

Model-specific implementations can be testable only when they make commitments such as:
- lattice/discretization;
- resource limits;
- topology;
- update rules;
- intervention constraints;
- implementation leakage.

Core rule:

TEST_OF_ONE_IMPLEMENTATION_FAMILY
!=
TEST_OF_ALL_POSSIBLE_SIMULATIONS.

## Observer-counting controls

Bostrom-style reasoning depends on:
- reference class;
- consciousness/substrate assumptions;
- simulator production rates;
- duplicate-counting rules;
- priors/model uncertainty.

Population arithmetic is not a measurement of our universe.

## Consciousness control

SIMULATED_BEHAVIOR

SIMULATION_COMPATIBILITY

## World Zero role

World Zero is useful for formal experiments about:
- provenance underdetermination;
- implementation leakage;
- intervention detection;
- what embedded agents can rationally infer.

Toy-world results cannot be promoted into evidence that our universe is simulated.
