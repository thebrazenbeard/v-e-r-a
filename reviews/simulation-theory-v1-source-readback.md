## Bostrom 2003

Direct author-hosted full text checked.

Confirmed:

- exact three-disjunct structure appears in the abstract and conclusion;
- substrate independence is explicitly introduced as an assumption;
- Bostrom says the needed assumption is weaker than unrestricted functionalism/computationalism;
- the core population model uses posthuman survival, interest in ancestor simulation, and simulated-observer fraction;
- the Bland Principle of Indifference is an explicit step;
- Bostrom discusses selective/demand-driven simulation, stacked virtual machines, ethical convergence, and loose religious analogies.

The On-Theo packets accurately preserve the distinction between Bostrom's argument and the separate claim that our universe is actually simulated.

Status: `PASS`.

Source: https://simulation-argument.com/simulation/

## Kipping 2020

Open arXiv abstract/full preprint record checked.

Confirmed:

- Kipping explicitly argues that uncertainty over whether Bostrom-like simulations are technically possible must be included in statistical treatment;
- he uses Bayesian model averaging;
- the reported probability remains below 50 percent and tends toward 50 percent in the limit of infinitely many simulations under the model;
- he argues actual human production of such simulations would radically change the odds.

The repo correctly labels this result `MODEL-CONDITIONAL` rather than a measured cosmic probability.

Status: `PASS`.

## Vazza 2025

Open arXiv full text checked.

Confirmed:
