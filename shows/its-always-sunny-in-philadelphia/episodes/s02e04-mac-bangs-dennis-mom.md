## Synopsis / analysis
Barbara Reynolds seduces Mac largely to hurt Frank. Charlie learns about it and turns the information into a revenge machine: Dennis is threatening to sleep with the Waitress unless Charlie resumes the degrading maintenance work Frank assigned him, so Charlie encourages Mac's affair, then orchestrates opportunities for Dennis to retaliate with Mrs. Mac and later Bonnie Kelly. Mrs. Mac rejects Dennis outright, puncturing his sexual self-concept. Dee and the Waitress become co-conspirators observing the retaliation chain. Barbara reveals Mac was only a weapon against Frank; the Waitress ultimately sleeps with Frank to get back at Dennis, leaving Charlie devastated by the consequences of the web he designed.

This is an early pure-form Sunny escalation structure: one grievance produces a retaliation graph in which sex is treated as a weapon, almost nobody is motivated by genuine attraction, and each character cares about what another person's desire says about their own status. Dennis's disbelief that Mrs. Mac does not find him attractive is especially foundational.
