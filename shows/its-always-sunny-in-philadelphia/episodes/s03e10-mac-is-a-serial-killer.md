A real serial killer is targeting women, and Mac's secretive behavior makes part of the Gang suspect him. Mac is actually reconnecting with Carmen. While one faction surveils Mac, another arrogantly decides it can profile and catch the killer. Their amateur investigation puts them near the genuine perpetrator while their internal certainty keeps shifting targets.

The episode is a compact demonstration of the Gang's epistemology: suspicion becomes proof when it is emotionally satisfying. Mac's desire to hide Carmen from their ridicule accidentally generates the exact secrecy they read as homicidal. Carmen's return also develops Mac's unresolved attraction-versus-identity conflict. The Gang's crime-solving confidence expands a pattern already seen in “Gun Fever”: they repeatedly appoint themselves investigators with no competence or mandate.
