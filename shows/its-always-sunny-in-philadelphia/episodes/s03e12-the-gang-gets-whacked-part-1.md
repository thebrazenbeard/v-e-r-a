# S03E12 — The Gang Gets Whacked, Part 1

Sources: Springfield `s03e12`; Wikipedia season 3.

The Gang finds cocaine hidden in speakers and, rather than alerting anyone, immediately tries to monetize it. The drugs belong to local mobsters, who impose a debt. The Gang responds by buying more cocaine to sell at a country club, treating a criminal threat like a badly managed small-business opportunity.

This is Sunny's scheme recursion in especially pure form: a failed illegal shortcut is “solved” by a larger version of the same shortcut. The country club matters because the Gang imagines wealthier customers as safe profit while failing to understand either narcotics distribution or organized crime. Part 1 establishes the debt structure and splits the group into strategies that become increasingly degrading in Part 2.
