# S03E15 — The Gang Dances Their Asses Off

Sources: Springfield `s03e15`; Wikipedia season 3.

Charlie's illiteracy causes him to enter Paddy's itself as the prize in a radio dance marathon. The Gang must compete to retain the bar, and familiar enemies also enter. What should produce teamwork instead becomes sabotage, alliance-shifting and individual attempts to secure ownership.

The season closes by making Paddy's literally contestable property and asking whether the Gang can cooperate when their shared habitat is at stake. Their answer is barely. Charlie's literacy problem is not a decorative trait here; it creates the contractual crisis. The marathon also functions as a continuity convergence point, bringing prior antagonists into one competitive space and showing that the Gang's accumulated social damage has produced a community of people happy to take their bar.
