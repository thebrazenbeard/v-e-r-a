The Gang is allowed back into a flip-cup competition after a long ban and becomes obsessed with defeating a college rival. Their remembered greatness is revealed as destructive drunken behavior rather than athletic honor. They eventually turn the rivalry into vandalism and humiliation. Season 5 ends with a recurring Sunny proposition: nostalgia never teaches them that the old behavior was the problem; it makes repetition feel like restoration.
