The Gang reveals the elaborate homegrown board/drinking game Chardee MacDennis. Its rules encode their relationship history: trivia, humiliation, physical pain, cheating disputes, alcohol and team identities are formalized into ritual. Dennis/Dee dominate Mac/Charlie partly because they understand and exploit the system better. The episode is unusually valuable as a map of internal governance—the Gang has created a complex institution, but its purpose is to preserve rivalry rather than fairness.
