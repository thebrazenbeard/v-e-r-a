Charlie is recruited for an intelligence experiment and becomes convinced that a pill has unlocked extraordinary cognitive ability. His speech, interests and self-presentation rapidly reorganize around the identity of a misunderstood genius, while the Gang reacts to losing the familiar version of him. The experiment's reveal undercuts the transformation without making Charlie's performance meaningless: expectation and status permission let him inhabit a different persona. The episode treats intelligence as both measurable capacity and socially performed identity, with Charlie especially vulnerable to the latter.
