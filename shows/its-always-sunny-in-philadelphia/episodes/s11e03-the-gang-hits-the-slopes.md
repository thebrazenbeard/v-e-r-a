A ski trip becomes an explicit 1980s ski-movie fantasy, complete with rival mountain cultures, reckless partying and genre logic the Gang enthusiastically inhabits. Dennis tries to impose a more realistic frame but is repeatedly dragged back into the fantasy. The episode is useful as a genre experiment: Sunny's characters do not merely reference movies; they use borrowed narrative rules to justify behavior and then act surprised when ordinary reality does not cooperate.
