Mac and Dennis accept a bet that they can live peacefully in the suburbs. Isolation strips away the bar's familiar outlets and turns their codependence into a domestic pressure cooker: Mac performs homemaking and reassurance while Dennis's commute, noise sensitivity and need for control become rage. The experiment demonstrates that Paddy's is not causing their dysfunction so much as containing it; remove the social environment and the same relationship dynamics become harder to disguise.
