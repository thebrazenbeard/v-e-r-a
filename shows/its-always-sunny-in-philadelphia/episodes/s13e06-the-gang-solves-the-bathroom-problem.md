Paddy's bathroom signs trigger a debate about sex, gender, identity and customer categories. The Gang approaches the issue as a taxonomy problem whose correct labels should eliminate social conflict, then discovers that every classification creates new edge cases or opportunities for insult. The episode's real subject is their hunger for a rule system that removes judgment from human interaction; their proposed solutions fail because people cannot be reduced to the categories they want to optimize.
