The Gang reconstructs its activities during 2020 and repeatedly discovers that private schemes intersected with highly visible public events in ways they did not understand at the time. The retrospective form lets them cast themselves as hidden historical actors while also exposing how little grasp they had of the larger systems around them. Continuity is deliberately braided with real-world chronology: their self-importance is funniest when an accidental effect is real but the meaning they assign to it is wrong.
