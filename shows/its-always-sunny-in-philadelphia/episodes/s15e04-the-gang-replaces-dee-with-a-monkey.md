While Dee pursues an acting opportunity, the men replace her bar role with a trained monkey and quickly project competence, friendship and loyalty onto it. The arrangement ends in intoxication, missing property and uncertainty about what actually happened during their blackout. The monkey works as a replacement test similar to Cindy and the Dennis doll: the Gang wants interchangeable functions until the substitute begins exercising agency outside their expectations.
