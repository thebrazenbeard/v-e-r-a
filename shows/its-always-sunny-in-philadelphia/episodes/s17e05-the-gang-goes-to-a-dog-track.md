Frank tricks the Gang into a trip to a closing dog track, where Dennis and Dee are pulled into gambling while Mac and Charlie meet Sparky, a philosophical janitor whose life is organized around the dogs. What begins as nostalgia and betting becomes progressively darker, culminating in Frank revealing that much of the trip was itself a wager about what he could induce the others to do. The episode treats Frank's manipulation as experimental: people become proof that his cynical model of them is correct.
