## Bibliography maintenance rules

1. Prefer DOI / PubMed / publisher page over secondary summaries when available.
2. Record sample and design in `docs/01-evidence-map.md` before promoting a finding into a strong working claim.
3. Do not treat media criticism as empirical evidence.
4. Do not infer universal sex differences from one mating-context study.
5. Mark preprints and repository-only manuscripts clearly.
6. Add contradictory studies, not just confirming ones.
7. Preserve null and mixed findings—they are part of the model.
