# Experimental research program

The general simulation hypothesis may be metaphysically underdetermined, but many **behavioral consequences of different world/controller architectures are modelable**.

This document connects `on-theo` to the originating interest in building our own World3-style systems model.

## Research question

Instead of asking only:

> Is God a computer program?

ask experimentally:

> If intelligent agents lived inside a governed simulated world, what observations and social histories would cause them to infer concepts resembling God, providence, revelation, miracle, judgment, free will, hidden reality or atheism?

## Minimal model layers

### Environment

A finite dynamic world with:

- resources;
- population/reproduction;
- mortality;
- environmental feedback;
- scarcity and abundance;
- shocks;
- spatial/social structure.

A World3-like ecological/economic kernel can provide macro constraints without being treated as a theology engine.

### Agents

Agents should have:

- partial local observation;
- memory;
- social learning;
- trust/reputation;
- model-building capacity;
- mortality awareness;
- ability to transmit stories across generations;
- imperfect copying and reinterpretation;
- incentives that can conflict with truth-seeking.

### Controller

Run multiple controller conditions rather than one "God" assumption.

#### C0 — no controller intervention

Environment follows laws after initialization.

#### C1 — omniscient observer, no intervention

Controller records everything but never changes state.

#### C2 — lawful intervention

Controller only acts through mechanisms available inside normal causal rules.

#### C3 — privileged intervention

Controller may perform state changes impossible for ordinary agents.

#### C4 — revelation-only

Controller can transmit information but cannot directly alter other world state.

#### C5 — minimal providence

Controller intervenes only to prevent specified catastrophic conditions.

#### C6 — evaluative/test controller

Controller has an explicit objective concerning agent behavior or civilization development.

#### C7 — experimenter with unknown objective

Controller acts according to a goal agents cannot observe.

#### C8 — deceptive/hostile controller

Useful as an adversarial comparison; avoids assuming power implies benevolence.

## Observation conditions

Give agents different evidence regimes:

- perfectly lawful world;
- rare genuine interventions;
- random anomalies indistinguishable from interventions;
- accurate revelation;
- noisy revelation;
- contradictory claimed revelations;
- privileged messages that later copy imperfectly;
- no revelation but strong human pattern-seeking.

## Questions to measure

- Do religions emerge without a controller?
- Can agents statistically distinguish interventions from rare natural events?
- What happens to a true message after 10, 50, 100 generations of oral/institutional transmission?
- Do institutions preserve or distort original information?
- Under what conditions do rival canons form?
- Do marginalized witnesses preserve useful information or merely generate alternative mythology at similar rates?
- When does authority status become mistaken for evidence quality?
- Can an in-world AI infer the controller more accurately than biological agents, or is that assumption anthropomorphic in reverse?
- Does knowledge of world mechanics increase atheism, theology, or simply more sophisticated theology?

## Jesus/Yeshua experiment class

Create a charismatic agent who receives one of several inputs:

- no external information, only unusual introspection;
- accurate cross-layer information translated into locally available symbols;
- noisy cross-layer information;
- ordinary apocalyptic/cultural inheritance;
- social stigma followed by intense self-modeling;
- combinations of the above.

Then observe whether later communities can reconstruct which condition actually occurred.

The goal is **not** to simulate historical Jesus and announce which run matches Christianity. The goal is to test how recoverable an originating insight remains after social transmission.

## Mary / authority experiment class

Model an early high-authority witness whose later reputation is altered by:

- gender hierarchy;
- sexualized rumor;
- institutional canon selection;
- rival authority networks;
- deliberate discrediting;
- accidental conflation with another person.

Measure how each mechanism affects preservation of that witness's original information.

## Panthera experiment class

Test how hostile counter-traditions evolve:

- true suppressed fact;
- invented polemical reversal;
- pun that later becomes biography;
- real person's name attached to unrelated scandal;
- ambiguous rumor recursively sharpened by both sides.

This is directly applicable to evaluating why a named counter-story can survive without proving its historical core.

## Qur'anic-late-transmission experiment class

Introduce a new community six centuries after the original event with access to:

- descendants of multiple prior traditions;
- canonical texts;
- noncanonical stories;
- oral preaching;
- polemics;
- no direct original witnesses.

Then test what types of earlier information can remain accurate, which are likely to be transformed, and how corrective revelation claims affect the information ecosystem.

## Falsification value

These simulations cannot prove our universe is simulated or identify a true religion.

They **can** test weaker claims such as:

- whether certain transmission patterns are plausible;
- whether a hidden controller would be inferable;
- whether intervention frequency affects religious certainty;
- how quickly source distinction collapses;
- whether "privileged" agents are distinguishable from lucky/confident ones;
- how social power changes which historical narrative survives.

That converts parts of `on-theo` from metaphysical storytelling into explicit systems hypotheses.
