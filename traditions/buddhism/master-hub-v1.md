# Buddhism — Master Research Lane V1

Status: FOUNDATION / DIAchronic reconstruction required

## Scope

This branch reconstructs Buddhist traditions from early textual strata through later schools without treating "Buddhism" as one timeless doctrine.

Core guard:

EARLY_BUDDHIST_TEXTS != ALL_LATER_BUDDHISM.

## Source architecture

### Early discourse and monastic collections

SuttaCentral provides parallel access to:
- the four major Pali Nikāyas;
- Vinaya material;
- Chinese Āgama parallels;
- Sanskrit fragments;
- Tibetan parallels where available.

Source:
https://suttacentral.net/introduction

Research rule:
parallel transmission across Pali, Chinese, Sanskrit, Gandhari, and Tibetan witnesses is more probative than treating one late received canon as the sole original.

### Pali canon

Traditional structure:
- Vinaya Piṭaka;
- Sutta Piṭaka;
- Abhidhamma Piṭaka.

The four large Nikāyas form the core of the discourse collection; the Khuddaka collection is heterogeneous.

Source-control note:
the Pali canon was transmitted orally before manuscript preservation. Traditional Sri Lankan chronology for writing the canon down must be treated separately from composition dates of individual texts.

## Historical development lanes

### Early communities
- śramaṇa environment;
- early Saṅgha;
- royal/political patronage;
- Aśokan period;
- early schools.

### Abhidharma
Do not project later scholastic ontologies backward into the earliest discourse strata without textual control.

### Mahāyāna
Track:
- earliest manuscript evidence;
- Prajñāpāramitā;
- bodhisattva path;
- Madhyamaka;
- Yogācāra;
- Buddha-nature traditions.

### Vajrayāna / Tantra
Keep tantric ritual, deity yoga, mandala, mantra, and subtle-body systems periodized separately.

### East Asian developments
Track Chinese translation history and local developments:
- Tiantai;
- Huayan;
- Chan;
- Pure Land.

### Tibetan developments
Keep Indian source transmission, Tibetan translation, indigenous system-building, and later scholastic classifications distinct.

## Comparative controls

Do not flatten:
- nirvāṇa into "heaven";
- karma into divine reward/punishment;
- rebirth into a permanent soul doctrine;
- śūnyatā into simple nihilism;
- buddha-nature into a universal creator deity;
- meditation into one uniform practice.
