# Daoism / Taoism — Master Research Lane V1

Status: FOUNDATION / PHILOSOPHICAL-RELIGIOUS DIAchronic CONTROL

## Naming

"Daoism" covers multiple historical phenomena.

Core guard:

CLASSICAL_DAOIST_TEXTS != ALL_LATER_DAOIST_RELIGION.

Do not reduce the tradition to "Laozi said X."

## Early textual lane

### Laozi / Daodejing

Current scholarship treats the received Laozi as a composite textual tradition rather than a securely authored sixth-century-BCE book by one historical individual.

Manuscript evidence from Guodian and Mawangdui demonstrates early textual variation.

Source:
https://plato.stanford.edu/entries/laozi/

Research rule:
RECEIVED_81_CHAPTER_TEXT != SINGLE_ORIGINAL_ARCHETYPE.

### Zhuangzi

The Zhuangzi is also layered.

The Inner, Outer, and Miscellaneous chapters have different authorship histories and should not be flattened into one author's systematic doctrine.

Source:
https://plato.stanford.edu/entries/zhuangzi/

## Religious Daoism

Religious Daoism developed through multiple lineages and revelation traditions.

The Stanford Encyclopedia's religious-Daoism treatment explicitly warns that continuity between Laozi/Daodejing and later organized forms is historically constructed in multiple ways.

Source:
https://plato.stanford.edu/entries/daoism-religion/

Priority historical lanes:
- Huang-Lao;
- Celestial Masters;
- Shangqing;
- Lingbao;
- monastic institutions;
- ritual registers;
- talismans;
- inner and outer alchemy;
- later Quanzhen and other movements.

## Buddhism / Daoism contact

Buddhism's transmission into China materially changed Chinese religious vocabulary and institutions.

Comparison must distinguish:
- translation borrowing;
- conceptual accommodation;
- polemic;
- institutional imitation;
- genuinely earlier Chinese ideas.

Guard:

SIMILARITY_BETWEEN_DAOISM_AND_BUDDHISM
!=
PRE-BUDDHIST_SHARED_DOCTRINE.

## Cosmology / simulation-theory guard

Dao, emptiness, spontaneity, transformation, dream motifs, and non-fixed distinctions may be useful comparative material.

ZHUANGZI_DREAM != SIMULATION_EVIDENCE
