# Islam lane — source spine

## Primary Qur'anic passages

Use a reliable Arabic text/translation and preserve passage numbers even when comparing translations.

### Creation / humanity

- Q 2:30–39 — Adam, angels, earthly role, knowledge, fall/guidance.
- Q 3:59 — comparison of Jesus and Adam.
- Q 30:30 — fitrah / created human disposition.
- Q 36:82 and related "Be, and it is" passages.
- Q 67:2 — life and death created as a test.
- Q 57:20 — provisional/deceptive character of worldly life.

### Mary / Jesus

- Q 3:42–59 — Mary, annunciation, Jesus, miracles, Adam comparison.
- Q 4:157–171 — crucifixion dispute; Messiah/Word/Spirit language; rejection of "Three."
- Q 5:110 — miracles "by My permission," including clay-bird motif.
- Q 19:16–36 — Mary, conception, accusation, speaking infant, servant/prophet language.
- Q 21:91 — Mary and son as a sign.

### Resurrection / judgment

Relevant passages are distributed throughout the Qur'an; index creation/re-creation, dead earth revived by rain, books/records, scales, witnesses, bodily testimony, resurrection and the Last Day separately rather than forcing one synthetic narrative.

## Transmission cautions

- A Qur'anic parallel to a noncanonical Christian story does not by itself identify the exact transmission channel.
- Later hadith/tafsir explanations should not be silently substituted for Qur'anic wording.
- The widespread later substitution-crucifixion scenario is not spelled out by Q 4:157 itself.
- Comparative work should distinguish theological correction, shared tradition, literary reuse, oral circulation, and independent structural similarity.
