# Judaism lane — source spine

This is a seed bibliography, not an exhaustive literature review.

## Primary / ancient texts

- Hebrew Bible / Tanakh: Daniel 7; Daniel 12; Job 1–2; Deuteronomy 6; Leviticus 19; relevant Psalms and prophetic restoration texts. Sefaria: https://www.sefaria.org/texts/Tanakh
- Josephus: useful but interested witness for first-century Jewish groups, rulers, unrest, Pilate, John the Baptist and other figures.
- Dead Sea Scrolls / Qumran corpus: primary evidence for Second Temple Jewish diversity, apocalypticism, purity, community identity and scriptural interpretation.
- Babylonian Talmud, especially traditions later associated with `ben Stada` / `ben Pandera`: late rabbinic evidence; not transparent first-century biography.
- Origen, *Contra Celsum*: principal surviving conduit for Celsus's otherwise lost anti-Christian work and its Panthera claim.

## Panthera / counter-tradition

- Lincoln Blumell, **A Jew in Celsus' True Doctrine? An examination of Jewish Anti-Christian polemic in the second century C.E.**: https://journals.sagepub.com/doi/10.1177/000842980703600206
  - Useful because it treats the uncertainty over whether Panthera is wordplay or an actual-name tradition and discusses the Abdes Pantera inscription.
- Jewish Virtual Library / *Encyclopaedia Judaica* entry on **Ben Stada**: https://jewishvirtuallibrary.org/jsource/judaica/ejud_0002_0003_0_02546.html
  - Useful especially for the chronological contradictions that make a simple identification with Jesus unsafe.

## Source cautions

- Josephus, Gospels, rabbinic literature, Christian apologists and anti-Christian polemicists all have interests and agendas.
- A tradition's hostility does not make it false; hostility also does not make it secretly true.
- Later rabbinic material may preserve earlier fragments, but each such claim needs argument rather than assumption.
