# Ásatrú / Norse Religion — Master Research Lane V1

Status: FOUNDATION / SOURCE-SEPARATION REQUIRED

## Scope

This branch distinguishes two related but non-identical subjects:

1. HISTORICAL_NORSE_RELIGION — pre-Christian and conversion-period North Germanic religious traditions reconstructed from medieval texts, archaeology, inscriptions, place-names, law, and comparative evidence.
2. MODERN_ASATRU — modern revival/reconstruction movements, including Icelandic Ásatrúarfélagið and other contemporary Heathen traditions.

Core guard:

MODERN_ASATRU != UNBROKEN_DIRECT_SURVIVAL_OF_VIKING_AGE_RELIGION.

Modern practitioners may intentionally draw on medieval sources and cultural inheritance, but historical continuity must be demonstrated rather than assumed.

## Primary textual source stack

### Poetic Edda

The Codex Regius of the Poetic Edda, GKS 2365 4to, was written around 1270.

It preserves mythological poems about gods and heroic poems, many of which are not preserved in other medieval manuscripts.

Research value:
PRIMARY_TEXT for medieval preservation of older poetic material.

Evidence ceiling:
MEDIEVAL_MANUSCRIPT_WITNESS_OF_TRADITIONS_WITH_EARLIER_CONTENT.

Not:
direct Viking-Age manuscript.

Institutional control:
https://arnastofnun.is/en/news/new-electronic-edition-codex-regius-poetic-edda

### Snorri's Edda

Snorri Sturluson's Edda is a thirteenth-century Christian-Icelandic learned work preserving extensive mythological material in a poetics/kenning framework.

Research rule:
SNORRI_TESTIMONY = LATER_MEDIEVAL_RECONSTRUCTION_AND_PRESERVATION.

It must be compared against Eddic poetry, skaldic verse, archaeology, and inscriptions before being projected backward.

## Modern Ásatrú lane

Ásatrúarfélagið states that it was founded in Iceland in 1972 and legally recognized in 1973.

Its own current presentation emphasizes:
- respect for old cultural heritage and nature;
- personal responsibility;
- tolerance;
- contemporary ritual/community activity.

Source:
https://asatru.is/
https://asatru.is/um-asatruarfelagid/

Modern institutional claims belong to:
LIVING_TRADITION_SELF_DESCRIPTION.

They do not automatically reconstruct historical Norse religion.

## Comparative controls

Do not flatten:
- Odin into a generic "sky father";
- Thor into every storm deity;
- Ragnarök into every apocalypse;
- Baldr into every dying-and-rising-god model;
- seiðr into generic "shamanism";
- Valhöll into a universal Norse afterlife.

Any transmission claim requires:
chronology + contact + carrier + semantic fit + source-specific evidence.
