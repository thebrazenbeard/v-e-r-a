## Internal variant control

Nihon shoki preserves alternative accounts in the Divine Age material.

This is positive evidence of plural source traditions.

Rule:
VARIANT_ACCOUNT != ERROR_TO_HARMONIZE.

Kojiki and Nihon shoki genealogies and deity sequences should be compared explicitly.

Guard:
MEDIEVAL_SHINTO != PURELY_ISOLATED_PRE_BUDDHIST_SYSTEM.

- State Shinto;

## High-value source questions

1. Earliest attestation of each kami.
2. Kojiki/Nihon shoki variant genealogy.
3. political compilation context.
4. shrine cult evidence independent of mythic text.
5. Buddhist reinterpretation.
6. modern ideological reconstruction.
